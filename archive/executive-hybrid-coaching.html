OmniFit's Executive Hybrid Coaching is a 12-week performance consulting program for San Diego professionals 30+ who want expert in-person coaching without daily gym commitments. Clients train in-studio for one 60-minute session every two weeks with a NASM-certified coach who holds corrective exercise (CES) and orthopedic exercise credentials. Between sessions, clients receive custom app programming, travel-adapted workouts for hotel and home gyms, form review videos, nutrition guidance, and direct messaging access to their coach. The program is designed for self-directed executives who execute independently but want structured expert oversight, periodic hands-on assessment, and full accountability.

How It Works

Two Touchpoints. One Integrated System.

Your program operates on two layers: deep in-person sessions every two weeks, and continuous async coaching in between. The sessions build your foundation. The async layer keeps you progressing daily.

01

In-Person Sessions (Every 2 Weeks)

60 minutes, 1:1 at our Pacific Beach studio. Your coach assesses movement quality, corrects form hands-on, tests strength benchmarks, and builds your next two-week training block.

  • Movement screening and posture assessment
  • Hands-on manual cueing and corrections
  • Strength and mobility benchmarking
  • Next-phase programming built live
  • 60-minute deep session, no shortcuts
02

Async Coaching (Between Sessions)

Between visits, you execute your custom program through the Trainerize app. Your coach monitors completion, reviews form videos, and adjusts workouts for travel weeks.

  • Custom training program in the app
  • Travel and hotel-adapted workout variations
  • Form review videos with coaching notes
  • Nutrition guidance and meal frameworks
  • Direct messaging — same-day response

OmniFit's Executive Hybrid Coaching operates on a performance consulting model rather than a traditional session-based model. Clients attend one 60-minute in-person session every two weeks for hands-on movement assessment, strength coaching, and program design, then execute their custom training plan independently using the Trainerize app between visits. The coach monitors workout completion, reviews submitted form videos, adapts programming for travel and equipment changes, and provides nutrition guidance via direct messaging. This structure is designed for self-directed professionals 30+ who have the discipline to train independently but need expert programming, periodic assessment, and structured accountability to achieve sustainable results.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How does OmniFit's Executive Hybrid Coaching work?
You train in-person at our Pacific Beach studio for one 60-minute session every two weeks. Between sessions, you execute a custom training program through the Trainerize app — with travel-adapted variations, form review videos, nutrition guidance, and direct messaging access to your coach. It's a performance consulting model designed for executives who can train independently but need expert programming and periodic hands-on assessment.
What equipment do I need between sessions?
Minimal equipment works: a pair of dumbbells, a resistance band, and a mat. For hotel rooms, we program bodyweight-only and band-based workouts that require zero equipment. For home gyms, we scale to whatever you have.
How is this different from the 30-Minute Executive Reset?
The Executive Reset is high-frequency virtual coaching — 30-minute live sessions 2–3 times per week via video. Executive Hybrid is low-frequency, high-depth in-person coaching — one 60-minute hands-on session every two weeks with full async support between visits. Choose the Reset if you want daily virtual structure. Choose Hybrid if you want periodic expert assessment and prefer to execute independently.
Can I add extra sessions if I want more in-person time?
Yes. Additional in-person sessions are available at $175 each, and live virtual sessions at $125 each. Nutrition deep-dives and mobility assessments are also available by request. Add-ons are purchased per session — no package commitment required.
How much does Executive Hybrid Coaching cost?
Executive Hybrid Coaching is $697 per month for a 3-month commitment, paid upfront ($2,091 total). This includes 6 in-person sessions, full app programming, travel workouts, form review videos, nutrition guidance, and direct coach messaging. In-home sessions are available with a $50–75 travel fee per session.
Who is Executive Hybrid Coaching designed for?
Self-directed professionals 30+ who are disciplined enough to train independently but want expert programming, hands-on assessment, and structured accountability. Ideal for executives who travel frequently, have home or hotel gym access, and prefer a consulting relationship over a daily trainer. Not designed for beginners who need constant supervision or clients who won't train between sessions.
