Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every Solana Beach client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your program starts with a $110 Performance Diagnostic and a comprehensive movement screening that maps compensations, restrictions, and asymmetries before we design a single exercise.

Solana Beach residents tend to be active: surfing, cycling, running on the bluffs. That's a strength, but it also means accumulated wear patterns that go unaddressed. Tight hip flexors from cycling. Shoulder impingement from paddling. Knee strain from running on uneven terrain. Our corrective-first approach identifies and resolves these patterns before layering strength work on top.

The result: you keep doing what you love, but with a body that moves better, recovers faster, and resists injury.

What is the Performance Diagnostic?

The OmniFit Performance Diagnostic ($110) uses the BodyStat 1500 MDD, a medical-grade bioimpedance device, to establish your body composition baseline: fat mass, lean mass, and hydration markers. Combined with a 9-point movement screen, it gives us objective data to build your program from and to measure real progress against at rescans. If you continue into a training package, the diagnostic fee is credited in full toward a 3-month package.

What certifications does your trainer hold?

Nemezio LopezPerez holds NASM-CPT, NASM-CES (Corrective Exercise Specialist), NASM-PES (Performance Enhancement Specialist), NASM-BCS (Behavior Change Specialist), NASM-CNC (Certified Nutrition Coach), and the ACE Corrective Exercise Specialist credential, plus Precision Nutrition certifications. All programming is evidence-based and assessment-driven.

I surf and cycle regularly. Will strength training complement that?

Absolutely, and this is where corrective-first programming is especially valuable. Surfing and cycling create specific muscle imbalances (tight hip flexors, rounded shoulders, limited thoracic rotation) that eventually lead to pain and injury if left unaddressed. We build your strength program around your recreational activities, correcting the wear patterns they create while building the stability and power that makes them more enjoyable.

Can I train outdoors in Solana Beach?

Yes. Weather-permitting outdoor sessions are available at parks and open spaces near Solana Beach. We combine bodyweight work, resistance bands, and portable equipment with the corrective and strength protocols from your program. Outdoor sessions follow the same structured programming as indoor sessions. Nothing is improvised.
