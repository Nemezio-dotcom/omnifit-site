Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every Carlsbad client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your program starts with a $110 Performance Diagnostic and a comprehensive movement screening, not a workout. We map your compensations, restrictions, and asymmetries before designing your program.

Carlsbad professionals often share a common profile: successful, active enough to play golf or coach their kids' sports, but carrying years of accumulated desk damage, old sports injuries, and the gradual strength loss that comes with a demanding career. The nagging lower back. The shoulder that limits overhead movement. The knee that's "fine unless I run."

Our corrective-first approach addresses these issues head-on. We don't just program around pain. We identify the dysfunction causing it, resolve it systematically, and then build real strength on a stable foundation.

Questions About Training in Carlsbad

How does in-home personal training work in Carlsbad?

We bring all necessary equipment to your Carlsbad or La Costa home: adjustable dumbbells, resistance bands, suspension trainers, and mats, or build your program around your existing home gym. Sessions are 45–60 minutes, fully programmed from your assessment data, and all you need is about 8×8 feet of clear space. A per-session travel fee may apply depending on location. Full details on our in-home training page.

What does a personal trainer in Carlsbad cost?

OmniFit Performance publishes its rates in full. One-on-one coaching at Teqneeq runs $250 to $1,395 per month on a 3-month program depending on tier (Essential, Momentum, Performance, or Peak), or $290 to $1,625 month-to-month. In-home coaching across San Diego County starts at $335 per month on a 3-month program. Couples training starts at $325 per month. Session packs run $135 to $175 per session depending on pack size and location; single sessions are $165 at Teqneeq or $195 in-home. The fully virtual Executive Reset program runs $449 to $995 per month for coached tiers, with an async programming tier at $175 per month. Every client starts with a $110 Performance Diagnostic, credited in full toward a 3-month package. Note: Momentum tier and above requires a Teqneeq membership ($119 per month, paid to Teqneeq); Essential and session-pack clients train on sponsored guest passes. Full tables on the training rates page.

Do you serve the La Costa area specifically?

Yes. La Costa is included in our Carlsbad service area. The La Costa Resort & Spa area, La Costa Oaks, La Costa Valley, and surrounding neighborhoods are all within our service zone.

What is the Performance Diagnostic?

The OmniFit Performance Diagnostic ($110) uses the BodyStat 1500 MDD, a medical-grade bioimpedance device, to establish your body composition baseline: fat mass, lean mass, and hydration markers. Combined with a 9-point movement screen, it gives us objective data to build your program from and to measure real progress against at rescans. If you continue into a training package, the diagnostic fee is credited in full toward a 3-month package.

What certifications does your trainer hold?

Nemezio LopezPerez holds NASM-CPT, NASM-CES (Corrective Exercise Specialist), NASM-PES (Performance Enhancement Specialist), NASM-BCS (Behavior Change Specialist), NASM-CNC (Certified Nutrition Coach), and the ACE Corrective Exercise Specialist credential, plus Precision Nutrition certifications. All programming is evidence-based and assessment-driven.

I play golf regularly and have lower back stiffness. Can you help?

Absolutely. This is one of the most common presentations among Carlsbad clients. Golf requires significant thoracic rotation and hip mobility, both of which deteriorate with desk work. Our corrective-first approach restores the mobility your swing demands while building the core stability that protects your lower back through thousands of repetitions.

Is Carlsbad too far from your studio for regular training?

Our studio inside Teqneeq FHC at 10772 Thornmint Rd in 4S Ranch is about 25 minutes from most Carlsbad locations, doable for weekly sessions but not ideal for 2–3x/week frequency. That's why in-home training is our most popular format for Carlsbad clients. We also offer Hybrid & Virtual Coaching that combines periodic studio visits with app-based virtual programming.
