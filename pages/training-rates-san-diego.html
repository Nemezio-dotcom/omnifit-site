Frequently Asked

Questions About Programs

How much does personal training cost in San Diego?

Personal training rates in San Diego range from $60–$200 per session depending on trainer certifications, session format, and program structure. OmniFit Performance offers structured programs billed every 4 weeks, starting at $640 for in-studio training at Teqneeq FHC and $900 for in-home training, with a discounted three-cycle Foundation for new clients from $560 per cycle. Every program includes monthly clinical-grade body composition assessments, custom programming, nutrition guidance, and weekly accountability.

What's included in OmniFit Performance programs?

Every program includes a monthly OmniFit Performance Assessment with clinical-grade Bodystat diagnostics, custom programming based on the OmniFit 4-phase methodology (Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain), nutrition guidance and macro framework, full app access for workout tracking, weekly accountability check-ins, and direct trainer access. In-home programs include all equipment — no home gym required.

How does billing work?

3-month commitment programs are paid in full upfront at signup and lock in the lower per-month rate. Month-to-month programs are billed monthly via auto-pay at a higher rate and can be cancelled with 30 days written notice. There are no activation fees.

What are the program tiers?

OmniFit Performance offers three named programs: Momentum (1 session per week), Performance (2 sessions per week), and Peak (3 sessions per week). Each is available for both Teqneeq FHC in-studio and in-home training, individually or as a couple. New clients start on The Foundation, three consecutive 4-week cycles paid in full at enrolment; after that, or by direct entry with prior training experience, The Program bills every 4 weeks. All tiers include the same comprehensive inclusions — the difference is training frequency.

Do you offer online or virtual personal training?

Yes. The Executive Reset delivers live 1:1 virtual coaching — Gold at $500 per 4 weeks (1 session per week), Platinum at $750 (2 per week), and Black at $1,100 (3 per week). Async-only programming (Bronze) is available at $200 per 4 weeks. Virtual coaching is available nationwide.

What happens after I finish my 3-month program?

Graduates of an OmniFit program can continue with the Monthly Tune-Up — one monthly session, a reassessment on the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 and an hour of training, with programming delivered through the OmniFit app between visits. Pricing is $295 per month for Teqneeq members, $325 for non-members (day pass included, no facility membership required), or $375 in-home. Billing is month-to-month with no long-term commitment; cancel with 30 days notice.

Does OmniFit offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes, on the Executive Reset. It includes a 30-Day Fit Guarantee: if after your first 30 days you decide it isn't the right fit, you can cancel and receive a refund of the unused balance of your program. You keep the first month and everything you've learned. There is no compliance test to pass and nothing to negotiate.

What is the OmniFit Performance Assessment?

The OmniFit Performance Assessment is a clinical-grade diagnostic session using the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 for body composition analysis, plus blood pressure, SpO2, movement screening, and grip strength testing. It's available in-studio at Teqneeq FHC or in your home for $150, credited toward your first cycle. The assessment is then included monthly in all training programs at no additional cost. The Bodystat QuadScan 4000 holds CE certification as a Class IIa medical device under MDD 93/42/EEC and has FDA clearance. OmniFit Performance uses it to measure body composition. It is not used to diagnose, treat or assess any medical condition, and OmniFit is not a medical provider.

Can I bring a guest to my training session?

Yes. Clients on any named program (Momentum through Peak) can bring a guest for $90 per session. This is a great way for a spouse, friend, or colleague to experience OmniFit before committing to their own program.

Can I pause my program if I travel?

Yes. 3-month commitment programs include a pause option of up to 2 weeks per term. Your commitment extends by the same duration at no additional cost. Many clients also use the virtual option to continue training during travel instead of pausing.

Where does in-home training take place?

OmniFit Performance serves 4S Ranch, Rancho Santa Fe, Carmel Valley, Del Mar, Solana Beach, Encinitas, Carlsbad, and as far south as La Jolla. All equipment is provided — no home gym required. In-home programs start at $900 per 4 weeks, or $800 per cycle on the three-cycle Foundation.

Program pricing and availability are subject to change. Contact us for the most current rates.
