Frequently Asked

Questions About Programs

How much does personal training cost in San Diego?

Personal training rates in San Diego range from $60–$200 per session depending on trainer certifications, session format, and program structure. OmniFit Performance offers structured monthly programs starting at $225/month (Essential, 1 session every 2 weeks) for in-studio training at Teqneeq FHC, and $275/month for in-home training. Every program includes monthly clinical-grade body composition assessments, custom programming, nutrition guidance, and weekly accountability.

What's included in OmniFit Performance programs?

Every program includes a monthly OmniFit Performance Assessment with clinical-grade BodyStat diagnostics, custom programming based on the OmniFit 4-phase methodology (Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain), nutrition guidance and macro framework, full app access for workout tracking, weekly accountability check-ins, and direct trainer access. In-home programs include all equipment — no home gym required.

How does billing work?

3-month commitment programs are paid in full upfront at signup and lock in the lower per-month rate. Month-to-month programs are billed monthly via auto-pay at a higher rate and can be cancelled with 30 days written notice. There are no activation fees.

What are the program tiers?

OmniFit Performance offers four named programs: Essential (1 session every 2 weeks), Momentum (1 session per week), Performance (2 sessions per week), and Peak (3 sessions per week). Each tier is available for both Teqneeq FHC in-studio and in-home training, with 3-month commitment and month-to-month pricing options. All tiers include the same comprehensive inclusions — the difference is training frequency.

Do you offer online or virtual personal training?

Yes. The Executive Reset delivers live 1:1 virtual coaching — Gold at $399/month (1 session per week), Platinum at $599/month (2 per week), and Black at $899/month (3 per week). Async-only programming (Bronze) is available at $150/month. Virtual coaching is available nationwide.

What happens after I finish my 3-month program?

Graduates of any 3-month OmniFit program can continue with the Monthly Tune-Up — one private session per month plus asynchronous coaching, ongoing nutrition support, and direct text access to your coach. Pricing is $179/month for Teqneeq members, $225/month for non-members (guest passes included — no facility membership required), or $265/month in-home. Billing is month-to-month with no long-term commitment; cancel with 30 days notice.

Does OmniFit offer a money-back guarantee?

The Executive Reset includes a 30-Day Performance Guarantee: if after 30 days of full compliance you don't feel clearly stronger, more energized, and more in control, you can cancel and receive a full refund of the remaining program balance. You keep the first month and everything you've learned.

What is the OmniFit Performance Assessment?

The OmniFit Performance Assessment is a clinical-grade diagnostic session using the BodyStat 1500 MDD (Class IIa medical device) for body composition analysis, plus blood pressure, SpO2, movement screening, and grip strength testing. It's available in-studio at Teqneeq FHC or in your home at an introductory price of $90. The assessment is included monthly in all training programs at no additional cost.

Can I bring a guest to my training session?

Yes. Clients on any named package (Essential through Peak) can bring a guest for $50 per session. This is a great way for a spouse, friend, or colleague to experience OmniFit before committing to their own program.

Can I pause my program if I travel?

Yes. 3-month commitment programs include a pause option of up to 2 weeks per term. Your commitment extends by the same duration at no additional cost. Many clients also use the virtual option to continue training during travel instead of pausing.

Where does in-home training take place?

OmniFit Performance serves 4S Ranch, Rancho Santa Fe, Carmel Valley, Del Mar, Solana Beach, Encinitas, Carlsbad, and as far south as La Jolla. All equipment is provided — no home gym required. In-home programs start at $275/month (Essential) with a 3-month commitment.

Program pricing and availability are subject to change. Contact us for the most current rates.
