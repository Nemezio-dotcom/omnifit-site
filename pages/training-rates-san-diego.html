How OmniFit Is Structured

Two Ways to Train

New clients

The Foundation

The Foundation is how new clients start at OmniFit: three consecutive 4-week cycles (12 weeks), covered by one payment at signup, from $1,680 ($560 per cycle). OmniFit Performance Assessment

The OmniFit Performance Assessment is a clinical-grade diagnostic session using the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 for body composition analysis, plus blood pressure, SpO2, movement screening, and grip strength testing. It's available in-studio at Teqneeq FHC or in your home for $150, credited toward your first cycle. Reassessments are included in every program; a client returning after a lapse of 60 days or more books a new assessment. The Bodystat QuadScan 4000 holds CE certification as a Class IIa medical device under MDD 93/42/EEC and has FDA clearance. OmniFit Performance uses it to measure body composition. It is not used to diagnose, treat or assess any medical condition, and OmniFit is not a medical provider.

Worked example: Foundation Momentum to Program Peak

A client on studio Momentum on The Foundation ($560 per 4-week cycle, 4 sessions per cycle, three cycles) decides after the first cycle that they want to train three times a week. Studio Peak is only offered on The Program, at $1,800 per 4-week cycle, so The Foundation ends at the cycle boundary and the unused sessions become credit.

  • Foundation per-session rate: $560 ÷ 4 sessions = $140 per session
  • Unused Foundation sessions: 2 remaining cycles × 4 sessions = 8 sessions
  • Credit: 8 sessions × $140 = $1,120
  • First Peak cycle on The Program: $1,800 − $1,120 credit = $680 due

If the credit ever exceeds the first Program cycle, the remainder carries forward as session credit against the next cycle, never as cash. The figures above follow from the published rates on this page.

Frequently Asked

Questions About Pricing

How much does OmniFit cost per month?

The Program starts at $640 per 4-week cycle for studio Momentum, which is 4 sessions per cycle. OmniFit bills in 4-week cycles, not calendar months. In-home starts at $900 per cycle. New clients usually start on The Foundation from $560 per cycle, paid as one $1,680 payment covering three cycles. Studio clients also need a Teqneeq membership at $119 per month; Teqneeq bills monthly.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment?

Only on The Foundation: three 4-week cycles (12 weeks), paid in one payment at signup. The Foundation is non-refundable, and the 30-Day Fit Guarantee is the only exit. The Program has no fixed term. It is charged every 4 weeks and continues until you give 30 days' notice. Experienced clients can start on The Program directly at any tier.

What do I pay to start?

Your 45-minute consultation by video or phone is booked with a $30 deposit, refunded the same day you attend your consultation. The Performance Assessment is $150, credited toward your first cycle. To enrol on The Foundation you pay all three cycles at signup, from $1,680. On The Program you pay the first 4-week cycle, from $640. No slot is held without payment.

Can I cancel, and with how much notice?

The Program: yes, with 30 days' notice. Remaining credit is applied to the final cycle. The Foundation is non-refundable; the 30-Day Fit Guarantee is the only exit. After its third cycle you continue on The Program, which you can then end with 30 days' notice.

What happens when I travel?

Use the Travel Protocol. The Foundation includes 2 pause weeks across the course, with notice, and they extend the end date. The Program includes 6 pause weeks per 12 months, with 2 weeks' notice, credited against the next cycle. You can also reschedule a session with 24 hours' notice within the same cycle, or take a scheduled session live by video during a travel week, at no extra cost.

Can I pay per session?

Yes. A single session is $205 for Teqneeq members, $235 for studio non-members (Teqneeq day pass included) and $275 in-home, and expires 30 days from purchase. A 5-pack is $180, $210 or $250 per session and expires 8 weeks from purchase. These are sessions, not a coaching program.

Is the assessment credited?

Yes. The Performance Assessment is $150, credited toward your first cycle on The Foundation or The Program. It is separate from the 45-minute consultation, which is booked with a $30 deposit, refunded the same day you attend your consultation. A client returning after a lapse of 60 days or more books a new assessment.

Can I choose how much accountability I get?

Yes. Every tier of The Foundation, The Program and the Executive Reset includes the Accountability Dial at no price difference: Full Accountability (daily logging of nutrition, steps and sleep, a coach reply within 1 business day, and behavior-change coaching), Coached (a weekly written check-in with a coach reply, 1 to 3 habit targets, and a measurement review every 4 weeks) or Train Only (your sessions and app program, a one-tap weekly check-in, nutrition on request). Executive Reset Bronze, which has no live sessions, offers Coached or Train Only. You set it at the Performance Assessment and can change it at any 4-week cycle boundary, including during The Foundation. The Tune-Up has no setting; it is the graduation point at the end of the Dial. Tracking is a diagnostic tool, not a life sentence. We measure closely at the start so we can coach less over time. The goal is a client who no longer needs us every week.

Can I change my plan after I start?

Yes, at any 4-week cycle boundary. Your accountability setting can change in either product, including during The Foundation. On The Program you can move up or down a tier (Momentum, Performance or Peak) between cycles, with notice before the next cycle starts. During The Foundation you can step up, not down: the first 12 weeks are built as a block. If your new tier is only offered on The Program, your Foundation ends there, every unused session is credited at the Foundation per-session rate toward your first Program cycle, and you pay only the difference. There is no move from The Program into The Foundation.

Do I have to join Teqneeq?

Only for studio training. Studio Momentum and above, on The Foundation or The Program, requires a Teqneeq membership at $119 per month. Teqneeq bills monthly; it is not an OmniFit charge, and it includes facility access on non-training days. Studio couples need it for both partners. In-home and virtual clients don't, and studio non-member session prices include a day pass.

Program pricing and availability are subject to change. Contact us for the most current rates.
