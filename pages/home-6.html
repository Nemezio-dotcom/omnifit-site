Partner & Duo Training

One Session. Two People. Two Separate Programs.

Couples training at OmniFit is not two people doing the same workout. Both of you are screened separately and get your own program. What you share is the hour, the coach, and the habit of showing up. Available at Teqneeq in 4S Ranch or in your home across North County San Diego.

Two screens, two programs

Each partner completes their own Performance Assessment. Programming is written per person, not per couple, so a shoulder issue on one side never dictates the other's plan.

The session is split

Where goals or capacity differ, which is most of the time, you alternate through different work rather than sharing a set. One of you may be building a squat while the other is still correcting one.

Progress measured separately

You log your own weights and reps, and each of you gets your own 30-day progress report. Rates are per couple, not per person, but the data is never blended.

Couples personal training at OmniFit Performance is one coached session with two people in it, each following a separate program built from their own assessment. Sessions run at Teqneeq Functional Health Center in 4S Ranch or in-home across North County San Diego, and are priced per couple.
