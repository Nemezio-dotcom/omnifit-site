A San Diego Personal Trainer Who Measures First

OmniFit Performance is the San Diego personal training practice of Nemezio LopezPerez, NASM Elite Trainer, coaching since 2016. It is built for professionals 30+ across San Diego County who want joint-friendly strength, better posture and a plan that survives a full calendar. Every client works 1:1 with the same coach, and every program starts from a Performance Assessment instead of a template.

How it differs from a San Diego gym or class

A gym gives you access and leaves the program to you. A class runs one pace for the room. Here your program is written from your Performance Assessment and rebuilt every 4-week cycle. Progress is judged against your own baseline: Bodystat QuadScan 4000 body composition, nine movement benchmarks, posture photos and strength. Joints and old injuries are programmed for, with corrective work in every warm-up and loading under a 3/10 pain ceiling.
