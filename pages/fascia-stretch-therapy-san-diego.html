The Short Definition

What Fascia Stretch Therapy Is

Fascia Stretch Therapy (FST) is assisted stretching for mobility and recovery. You lie on a table and a trained practitioner does the stretching with you, so you are not working through a routine on your own. At OmniFit Performance it is delivered by Nemezio LopezPerez, Fascia Stretch Practitioner – Level 1 (Stretch to Win Institute), in clients' homes across North County San Diego. It is offered in-home only, it is open to anyone, and every new client starts with a 90-minute First Visit.
Where Everyone Starts

What the First Visit Includes

The First Visit is 90 minutes, costs $150 and is required for every new FST client. It has three parts.

Part 1
A range-of-motion check

Before anything else, Nemezio measures your range of motion. Those are your starting measurements.

Part 2
Your first session on the table

Your first Fascia Stretch Therapy session. You lie on the table he brings, and he guides the stretching from start to finish.

Part 3
A re-measure at the end

The same ranges are measured again before he leaves, and you see both sets of measurements.

The First Visit is not the Performance Assessment, and its $150 is not credited toward anything.

Questions

Fascia Stretch Therapy FAQs

What is Fascia Stretch Therapy?
Fascia Stretch Therapy (FST) is assisted stretching for mobility and recovery, done on a table: you lie down and a trained practitioner does the stretching with you. It is also written "fascial stretch therapy". At OmniFit Performance it is delivered in your home by Nemezio LopezPerez, Fascia Stretch Practitioner – Level 1 (Stretch to Win Institute).
What happens at the First Visit?
The First Visit is 90 minutes and is required for every new FST client. It has three parts: a range-of-motion check, your first Fascia Stretch Therapy session on the table, and a re-measure at the end, so you see the same ranges measured before and after. Follow-up sessions and 5-session packs are arranged with Nemezio after the First Visit.
How much does it cost?
The First Visit is $150 for 90 minutes. After that, a single 60-minute session is $225, and a 5-session pack is $200 per session ($1,000), which expires 8 weeks from purchase. These are in-home rates. Outside the no-fee part of our in-home service area a travel fee applies, confirmed before you book.
Do I need to be a training client?
No. Fascia Stretch Therapy is open to anyone. You do not need a training program or a Performance Assessment to book it. It stands on its own, and it can also pair with a training program if you have one.
Where do sessions take place and what do I need?
Sessions take place in your home, within OmniFit Performance's in-home service area in North County San Diego. You need a quiet room with floor space. The table comes with Nemezio. No travel fee in Carmel Valley, Santaluz, Fairbanks Ranch, Rancho Bernardo, 4S Ranch or Poway; elsewhere in our in-home service area a travel fee applies, confirmed before you book.
What should I wear?
Wear clothes you can move in. That is all you need to prepare: the table comes with Nemezio.
Is FST massage or medical treatment?
No. Fascia Stretch Therapy is assisted stretching for mobility and recovery. It is not medical treatment, not physical therapy and not massage.
What is the cancellation policy?
Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice and no-shows forfeit the fee.
