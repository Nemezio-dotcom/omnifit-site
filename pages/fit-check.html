Are you the right fit?

OmniFit is diagnostic-led coaching. We measure first, then build a program written for you. It works for people who are ready and able to start. Seven questions, about three minutes. Answer honestly, not the way you think you should. No answers are saved or sent; only an anonymous count of completed checks and their result is recorded. You'll see your result right away, including the accountability setting we'd recommend you start on.

Built for you if

  • You've decided, and you're starting within the month
  • You want a program written for you, not a template
  • You can budget for the published rates and keep your appointments

Not the right fit if

  • You mainly want group energy or the lowest price
  • You're still deciding whether it's worth it
  • The next three months leave no room for sessions

New to training? That's fine. The Performance Assessment sets your starting point, the first cycles are built around it, and corrective work sits in every warm-up. Even if you just want to train, we can start there.
