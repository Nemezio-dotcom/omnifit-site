The Practitioners Behind the Performance

OmniFit Performance partners with select board-certified clinicians and licensed therapists in San Diego to bridge the gap between rehabilitation, recovery, and long-term corrective strength training. Every referral is earned by outcomes, not affiliations.

Our Referral Philosophy

We don't partner with everyone. OmniFit's clinical partners are licensed professionals who share our commitment to evidence-based corrective work, measurable outcomes, and the belief that no one should be left in the gap between "cleared by PT" and "ready to train at full capacity." Our clients deserve practitioners who understand executive lifestyles, travel demands, and the value of their time.

The PT-to-Performance Bridge

Why Clinical Partnerships Matter at OmniFit

OmniFit Performance in San Diego maintains formal clinical partnerships with board-certified specialists and licensed therapists to ensure a seamless transition from rehabilitation and recovery to corrective strength training. Founding clinical partner Dr. Damien Jackson-Ricketts, DPT, OCS, operates Stoke Physical Therapy — a concierge practice serving active professionals and athletes. Recovery partner Celeste Esposito, LMT, operates Serenity Massage, providing therapeutic bodywork that addresses the soft tissue restrictions corrective exercise alone cannot resolve.

This "PT-to-Performance Bridge" model means post-rehab clients are never left without structured guidance after being cleared by their clinician — and clients with chronic tension, adhesions, or recovery demands have a direct pathway to targeted soft tissue therapy coordinated with their training program. Each partnership is built on shared assessment insights, coordinated treatment progressions, and bi-directional referral pathways. OmniFit's NASM Corrective Exercise Specialist (CES) methodology aligns directly with clinical orthopedic screening and therapeutic bodywork, creating continuity of care that typical personal training cannot provide.
