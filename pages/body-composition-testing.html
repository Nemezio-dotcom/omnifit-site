What the Scan Measures

Data a Bathroom Scale Will Never Give You

The scale reports one number and hides everything that matters. Two people can weigh exactly the same and have completely different bodies — and two "identical" weigh-ins a month apart can hide muscle loss behind water shifts. The BodyStat separates the signal from the noise.

⚖️

Fat Mass vs. Lean Mass

The core split: how much of your weight is body fat versus muscle, bone, and organ tissue. This is the number that tells you whether a diet is working — or just shrinking you.

💧

Hydration & Fluid Balance

Total body water plus the ratio of fluid inside versus outside your cells (ICW/ECW). Explains "mystery" weight swings and flags inflammation or under-recovery.

⚡

Phase Angle

A cellular-health marker measured directly by the device — higher values generally reflect stronger cell membrane integrity and better training readiness. Tracked at every rescan.

📐

FFMI & Trend Data

Fat-Free Mass Index puts your muscularity in context of your height — a far better benchmark than BMI. Every rescan builds your trend line, which is where the real answers live.

Common Questions

Body Composition Testing FAQ

How much does body composition testing cost in San Diego at OmniFit?
The Performance Diagnostic is $110 and includes the BodyStat 1500 MDD body composition scan, a 9-point movement screen, and expert interpretation of your results. If you continue into a 3-month package, the $110 is credited in full toward it. There are no activation fees.
What is the BodyStat 1500 MDD?
The BodyStat 1500 MDD is a medical-grade bioelectrical impedance analysis (BIA) device that measures body composition by passing a safe, imperceptible electrical signal through the body. It reports fat mass, lean mass, total body water, intracellular and extracellular water balance, and phase angle — a cellular health marker. It's the same class of device used in clinical and research settings, not a consumer gym kiosk.
How is this different from a DEXA scan?
DEXA uses low-dose X-ray imaging and is the research gold standard for a single-snapshot measurement, typically costing $100–200 per scan in San Diego. The BodyStat uses bioimpedance — no radiation — and adds hydration and cellular data DEXA doesn't measure. The bigger difference is context: OmniFit scans are interpreted by the coach who writes your program and repeated every 4–8 weeks on the same calibrated device, so your trend line drives real programming decisions rather than sitting in a PDF.
I'm on a GLP-1 medication. Why does body composition testing matter?
Rapid weight loss on GLP-1 medications can include significant lean mass loss, and a scale can't distinguish fat from muscle. Regular body composition testing shows exactly what kind of weight you're losing, so resistance training and protein intake can be adjusted to protect muscle — and gives your prescriber objective data for treatment decisions. This is one of the most common reasons clients book the assessment.
How should I prepare for the scan?
For the most accurate reading: avoid eating for 2–3 hours beforehand, skip alcohol for 24 hours, avoid intense exercise immediately before, and arrive normally hydrated. Consistency matters more than perfection — we schedule your rescans under similar conditions so your trend data stays clean.
How often should I rescan?
Every 4–8 weeks, depending on your goal. Body composition changes too slowly for weekly scans to mean anything, and quarterly gaps miss course-correction windows. Active OmniFit clients rescan as a built-in checkpoint — it's the accountability layer of the program, not an add-on.

No. The $110 assessment is available as a standalone service — you leave with your full results, expert interpretation, and a concrete plan either way. If you do continue into a training package, the fee is credited in full toward a 3-month package.
