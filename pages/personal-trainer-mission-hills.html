Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every Mission Hills client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your first session is a comprehensive movement screening that maps compensations, restrictions, and asymmetries before we program a single exercise.

Mission Hills professionals are typically established in their careers — attorneys, physicians, executives, business owners — commuting to downtown offices and managing high-stress workloads. The physical toll is predictable: forward head posture from years of screen time, tight hip flexors from extended sitting, lower back stiffness that gets worse every year, and the gradual loss of strength and energy that comes with a sedentary career pattern.

Our corrective-first approach breaks that cycle. We don’t just give you workouts — we identify the specific dysfunction your lifestyle has created, resolve it systematically, and build functional strength that carries into your workday, your weekends, and your long-term health.

Questions About Training in Mission Hills

How does in-home personal training work in Mission Hills?

We come to your Mission Hills home and train using your existing equipment or a minimal setup. Sessions are 45–60 minutes, fully programmed from your movement assessment. A $50 travel fee per session applies. Full details on our in-home training page.

What does a personal trainer in Mission Hills cost?

In-home sessions in Mission Hills are $200–225 per session ($150–175 base rate + $50 travel fee). Studio sessions at our Pacific Beach facility — 10 minutes away — start at $150/session with no travel fee. Executive Hybrid Coaching is $500/month for 12 weeks. See our training rates page for full details.

My Mission Hills home doesn’t have a gym. Can I still do in-home training?

It depends on your space. At minimum you need adjustable dumbbells, a bench, resistance bands, and about 8×8 feet of clear space — a spare room, garage, or covered patio works. If your space is limited, our Pacific Beach studio is just 10 minutes away and is the more practical option. We’ll help you assess during your consultation.

What certifications does your trainer hold?

Nemezio Lopez Perez holds NASM-CPT, NASM-CES (Corrective Exercise Specialist), NASM-PES (Performance Enhancement Specialist), NASM-BCS (Behavior Change Specialist), and NASM-CNC (Certified Nutrition Coach). All programming is evidence-based and assessment-driven.

I sit at a desk all day downtown and come home with back and neck pain. Can you help?

This is the most common issue we address among Mission Hills professionals. Hours of seated desk work create predictable postural dysfunction — forward head posture, thoracic kyphosis, hip flexor tightness, and core instability. Our corrective-first approach identifies exactly which patterns are causing your pain and resolves them systematically. Most clients see measurable improvement within 4–6 sessions.

Can I train before or after work to fit my downtown commute?

Absolutely. Many Mission Hills clients train early morning (6–7am) before heading downtown or evening (6–8pm) after returning. In-home sessions eliminate any additional commute. Studio sessions at our PB facility take 10 minutes each way. We build your schedule around your work life, not the other way around.
