Questions

HIIT Training FAQs

How is OmniFit HIIT different from F45 or Orangetheory?
F45 and Orangetheory are group classes with the same workout for everyone. OmniFit HIIT is private 1-on-1 coaching where your intervals, exercise selection, and intensity are customized to your body, injuries, and goals. We also build corrective exercise and mobility into every session.
Is HIIT safe if I have old injuries or joint problems?
Yes. Every session follows the 3/10 pain ceiling rule — if a movement causes more than mild discomfort, it's modified or replaced. The corrective warm-up addresses your specific limitations. High intensity doesn't have to mean high impact.
How often should I do HIIT?
For most professionals over 30, we recommend 2–3 HIIT sessions per week combined with 1–2 strength sessions. Your exact frequency is determined by your goals, recovery capacity, and overall programming.
Can I combine HIIT with OmniFit's strength training?
Absolutely — most OmniFit clients do both. Your HIIT and strength sessions are programmed together so they complement each other rather than competing for recovery. This is part of the Assess → Correct → Build → Sustain framework.
What should I bring to an outdoor HIIT session?
Water, sunscreen, and athletic shoes. We bring all equipment — kettlebells, bands, battle ropes, or whatever the session calls for. Studio sessions include everything.
