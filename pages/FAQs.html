Programs & Services
What kind of personal training does OmniFit offer?
OmniFit Performance offers three training formats, all designed for professionals and executives 30+. In-studio private training at Teqneeq FHC in 4S Ranch, fully private, one client at a time. In-home training throughout San Diego County (La Jolla, Del Mar, Rancho Santa Fe, Carmel Valley, Encinitas). And the 30-Minute Executive Reset, a fully virtual program with live sessions 2–3×/week. Every program follows our Assess → Correct → Build → Sustain methodology.
What is the 30-Minute Executive Reset?
The Executive Reset is a high-frequency virtual coaching program with live 30-minute sessions 2–3 times per week via video. It includes app-based programming, nutrition coaching, and habit systems. Designed for executives who want daily structure and accountability from anywhere: home, office, or hotel room.
Do you offer in-home personal training in San Diego?
Yes. In-home training is available throughout San Diego County including La Jolla, Del Mar, Rancho Santa Fe, Carmel Valley, and Encinitas. A per-session travel fee may apply depending on location. Your coach brings all necessary equipment. Programming is identical to our studio sessions, fully individualized, assessment-driven, with monthly progress reports.
How is OmniFit different from F45, Orangetheory, or Equinox personal training?
OmniFit Performance is a private 1:1 coaching practice, not a gym and not a group fitness studio. There are no classes, no shared sessions, and no rotating trainers. Your program is individually designed around your movement assessment, not adapted from a class template. OmniFit specializes in corrective exercise and executive scheduling flexibility, which group formats and large-gym personal training departments cannot accommodate. Training & Travel
What if I travel frequently for work?
Travel-adapted programming is built into every OmniFit Performance program. In-studio and in-home clients receive hotel and home gym workout variations through the app between sessions. Executive Reset clients train live via video from anywhere. In-person clients can add virtual sessions during travel weeks. No one loses momentum because of a flight.
What equipment do I need for virtual or hybrid sessions?
Minimal equipment works: a pair of dumbbells, a resistance band, and a mat. For hotel rooms, we program bodyweight-only and band-based workouts requiring zero equipment. For home gyms, we scale to whatever you have. Every program includes equipment-adaptive variations so you never miss a workout due to access limitations.
I have old injuries or joint pain. Can I still train?
Yes. OmniFit Performance specializes in joint-friendly, corrective-first training. Your coach holds NASM Corrective Exercise (CES) and ACE Corrective Exercise Specialist credentials. Every program begins with a 9-point movement screen and follows a 3/10 pain ceiling protocol, meaning we never push through pain above a 3 out of 10. OmniFit is not a medical provider, but we serve as a structured bridge between physical therapy discharge and full performance training. When appropriate, we coordinate with your PT or physician.
Results & Nutrition
What results can I expect?
Results vary by starting point and adherence, so OmniFit Performance measures every client against their own baseline rather than a promise. Compliant clients typically see movement quality and posture improve first, followed by measurable body composition change, strength increases, and better energy and sleep. OmniFit tracks progress with monthly data reports including body composition, strength benchmarks, posture photos, adherence metrics, and well-being scores. We don't promise miracles. We show you the data.
Is nutrition coaching included?
Yes. Every OmniFit Performance program includes nutrition guidance. You'll receive custom macros, simple meal frameworks, travel and dining strategies, and weekly accountability check-ins. We use a Precision Nutrition-informed approach focused on sustainable habit change rather than restrictive diets. For clients who want deeper nutrition support, dedicated nutrition coaching add-ons are available.
