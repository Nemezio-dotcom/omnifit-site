Programs & Services
What kind of personal training does OmniFit offer?
OmniFit Performance offers three training formats, all designed for professionals and executives 30+. In-studio training: private 1:1 coaching inside Teqneeq FHC, a members-only facility in 4S Ranch. In-home training across our North County San Diego service area (La Jolla, Del Mar, Rancho Santa Fe, Carmel Valley, Encinitas). And the 30-Minute Executive Reset, a fully virtual program with an async programming tier, or live 30-minute sessions one to three times a week. Every program follows our Assess → Correct → Build → Sustain methodology.
What is the 30-Minute Executive Reset?
The Executive Reset is a virtual coaching program with live 30-minute sessions 1–3 times per week via video, depending on tier. It includes app-based programming, nutrition coaching, and habit systems. Designed for executives who want structure and accountability from anywhere: home, office, or hotel room.
Do you offer in-home personal training in San Diego?
Yes. In-home training is available throughout San Diego County including La Jolla, Del Mar, Rancho Santa Fe, Carmel Valley, and Encinitas. No travel fee in Carmel Valley, Santaluz, Fairbanks Ranch, Rancho Bernardo, 4S Ranch or Poway; elsewhere in our in-home service area a travel fee applies, confirmed before you book. Your coach brings all necessary equipment. Programming is identical to our studio sessions, fully individualized, assessment-driven, with progress reviews every 4 weeks (at least every 12 weeks on Train Only).
How is OmniFit different from F45, Orangetheory, or Equinox personal training?
OmniFit Performance is a private 1:1 coaching practice, not a gym and not a group fitness studio. There are no classes, no shared sessions, and no rotating trainers. Your program is individually designed around your movement assessment, not adapted from a class template. OmniFit specializes in corrective exercise and executive scheduling flexibility, which group formats and large-gym personal training departments cannot accommodate. Training & Travel
What if I travel frequently for work?
Travel-adapted programming is built into every OmniFit Performance program. In-studio and in-home clients are on the Travel Protocol: hotel and home gym workout variations through the app between sessions, check-ins that continue at your Accountability Dial setting, and pause weeks when a trip can't hold training. Executive Reset clients train live via video from anywhere. In-person clients can take a scheduled session live by video during a travel week, at no extra cost. No one loses momentum because of a flight.
What equipment do I need for virtual or hybrid sessions?
Minimal equipment works: a pair of dumbbells, a resistance band, and a mat. For hotel rooms, the Travel Protocol programs bodyweight-only and band-based workouts requiring zero equipment. For home gyms, we scale to whatever you have. Every program includes equipment-adaptive variations so you never miss a workout due to access limitations.
I have old injuries or joint pain. Can I still train?
Yes. OmniFit Performance specializes in joint-friendly, corrective-first training. Your coach holds NASM Corrective Exercise (CES) and ACE Corrective Exercise Specialist credentials. Every program begins with a 9-point movement screen and follows a 3/10 pain ceiling protocol, meaning we never push through pain above a 3 out of 10. OmniFit is not a medical provider, but we serve as a structured bridge between physical therapy discharge and full performance training. When appropriate, we coordinate with your PT or physician.
Results & Nutrition
What results can I expect?
Results vary by starting point and adherence, so OmniFit Performance measures every client against their own baseline rather than a promise. Compliant clients typically see movement quality and posture improve first, followed by measurable body composition change, strength increases, and better energy and sleep. OmniFit tracks progress with progress reviews every 4 weeks covering body composition, strength benchmarks, posture photos, adherence metrics, and well-being scores. We don't promise miracles. We show you the data.
Is nutrition coaching included?
Yes. Every OmniFit Performance program includes nutrition guidance. You'll receive custom macros, simple meal frameworks, travel and dining strategies, and weekly accountability check-ins. We use a Precision Nutrition-informed approach focused on sustainable habit change rather than restrictive diets.
Accountability & Flexibility
Can I choose how much accountability I get?
Yes. Every tier of The Foundation, The Program and the Executive Reset includes the Accountability Dial at no price difference: Full Accountability (daily logging of nutrition, steps and sleep, a coach reply within 1 business day, and behavior-change coaching), Coached (a weekly written check-in with a coach reply, 1 to 3 habit targets, and a measurement review every 4 weeks) or Train Only (your sessions and app program, a one-tap weekly check-in, nutrition on request). Executive Reset Bronze, which has no live sessions, offers Coached or Train Only. You set it at the Performance Assessment and can change it at any 4-week cycle boundary, including during The Foundation. The Tune-Up has no setting; it is the graduation point at the end of the Dial.
Will I have to track food forever?
No. Tracking is a diagnostic tool, not a life sentence. We measure closely at the start so we can coach less over time. The goal is a client who no longer needs us every week. Full Accountability, with daily logging, is the default for the first cycles. The path from there is Full Accountability → Coached → Train Only → Tune-Up. On Coached you work on 1 to 3 habit targets instead of logging every meal, and on Train Only nutrition is on request.
How personalized is the app?
Sessions are for form, load and corrective work. The app carries your individual program between sessions, your logs and your check-ins — written for you, not a template. Your program is built from your Performance Assessment, reviewed against your own baseline, and you are reassessed at least every 12 weeks. The same app carries it whether you train in the studio, at home or virtually.
Is OmniFit too intense for a beginner?
No. The OmniFit Method measures first: the Performance Assessment sets your starting point, corrective work sits in every warm-up, and loading is chosen for what your joints tolerate under the 3/10 pain ceiling. The Foundation is a defined 12-week starter block built for new clients: three 4-week cycles, one payment, a known end date. Even if you just want to train, we can start there.
What if I travel or my schedule changes?
Pause weeks protect your slot: 2 across The Foundation with notice, extending the end date, and 6 per 12 months on The Program with 2 weeks' notice, credited against the next cycle. Sessions reschedule with 24 hours' notice within the same cycle, and sessions OmniFit cancels are credited. Studio, in-home and virtual run on one app, so your program travels with you on the Travel Protocol: you can take a scheduled session live by video during a travel week, at no extra cost. The 30-Minute Executive Reset is fully virtual.
Can I change my plan after I start?
Yes, at any 4-week cycle boundary. Your accountability setting can change in either product, including during The Foundation. On The Program you can move up or down a tier (Momentum, Performance or Peak) between cycles, with notice before the next cycle starts. During The Foundation you can step up, not down: the first 12 weeks are built as a block. If your new tier is only offered on The Program, your Foundation ends there, every unused session is credited at the Foundation per-session rate toward your first Program cycle, and you pay only the difference. There is no move from The Program into The Foundation.
Will I be able to train on my own eventually?
That is the goal. The path is Full Accountability → Coached → Train Only → Tune-Up. The Tune-Up is for graduates: one session per 4-week cycle, a reassessment on the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 and an hour of training, with programming delivered through the OmniFit app between visits. We measure closely at the start so we can coach less over time.
What happens when my program ends?
You graduate. Your Accountability Dial steps down as you need less coaching, Full Accountability → Coached → Train Only → Tune-Up, and the Tune-Up is the graduation point: one reassessment on the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 and an hour of training every 4 weeks, with your program in the app between visits. The goal is a client who no longer needs us every week.
Policies

The Foundation is paid in full at enrolment; The Program is billed every 4 weeks via auto-pay and can be cancelled with 30 days' notice.
