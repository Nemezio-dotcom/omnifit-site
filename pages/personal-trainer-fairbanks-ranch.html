Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every Fairbanks Ranch client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your engagement begins with a $110 Performance Diagnostic and a comprehensive movement screening: overhead squat assessment, posture documentation, joint range of motion testing, and nine total movement benchmarks.

Fairbanks Ranch clients typically share a profile: successful professionals in their 40s–60s who have invested in home gym equipment but lack the structured programming and expert coaching to use it effectively. Many are managing old injuries: a torn rotator cuff that was never fully rehabbed, chronic lower back stiffness from decades of desk work, or knee pain that limits everyday movement.

Our corrective-first approach addresses these issues systematically before adding training load. The result: clients who are stronger, leaner, and pain-free, often for the first time in years.

What equipment do I need at home?

None is required. We bring professional-grade portable equipment when needed, so all you need is about 8×8 feet of clear space. That said, most Fairbanks Ranch clients have extensive setups, and we design your program around whatever you have, recommending additions only when they'd genuinely help.

What is the Performance Diagnostic?

The OmniFit Performance Diagnostic ($110) uses the BodyStat 1500 MDD, a medical-grade bioimpedance device, to establish your body composition baseline: fat mass, lean mass, and hydration markers. Combined with a 9-point movement screen, it gives us objective data to build your program from and to measure real progress against at rescans. If you continue into a training package, the diagnostic fee is credited in full toward a 3-month package.

Country club trainers typically work with many members in a shared facility using general programming. OmniFit provides fully individualized, assessment-driven training. Your program is built from a $110 Performance Diagnostic and a 9-point movement screen specific to your body, tracked with monthly progress reports, and adjusted based on hard numbers.
