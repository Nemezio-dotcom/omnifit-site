Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every Del Mar client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your program starts with a $110 Performance Diagnostic and a comprehensive movement screening: overhead squat assessment, posture documentation, joint range of motion testing, and nine total movement benchmarks. Objective data before a single rep is programmed.

From there, we address what the assessment reveals before adding training load. Tight hip flexors from long commutes. Rounded shoulders from desk work. Knee discomfort from years of running on the bluffs. We fix the foundation first, then build strength on top of it.

For Del Mar professionals, in-home training is the most popular format. No driving to a gym, no parking, no wasted time. We arrive with everything, execute a pre-planned session, and you're back to your day in 70 minutes total.

Questions About Training in Del Mar

How does in-home personal training work in Del Mar?

We bring all equipment to your Del Mar home: adjustable dumbbells, resistance bands, suspension trainers, and mats. Sessions are 45–60 minutes, fully programmed based on your assessment data. A garage, patio, living room, or backyard with about 8×8 feet of clear space works perfectly. A per-session travel fee may apply depending on location.

What does a personal trainer in Del Mar cost?

OmniFit Performance publishes its rates in full. One-on-one coaching at Teqneeq runs $250 to $1,395 per month on a 3-month program depending on tier (Essential, Momentum, Performance, or Peak), or $290 to $1,625 month-to-month. In-home coaching across San Diego County starts at $335 per month on a 3-month program. Couples training starts at $325 per month. Session packs run $135 to $175 per session depending on pack size and location; single sessions are $165 at Teqneeq or $195 in-home. The fully virtual Executive Reset program runs $449 to $995 per month for coached tiers, with an async programming tier at $175 per month. Every client starts with a $110 Performance Diagnostic, credited in full toward a 3-month package. Note: Momentum tier and above requires a Teqneeq membership ($119 per month, paid to Teqneeq); Essential and session-pack clients train on sponsored guest passes. Full tables on the training rates page.

Do you only train clients in Del Mar?

No. Del Mar sits at the heart of our coastal concierge territory. We also serve Solana Beach, Encinitas, Carlsbad, La Jolla, Carmel Valley, Rancho Santa Fe, and 4S Ranch. Studio sessions are available inside Teqneeq FHC, a members-only functional health and performance lab at 10772 Thornmint Rd in 4S Ranch, San Diego, about 20 minutes from Del Mar via the 56.

What is the Performance Diagnostic?

The OmniFit Performance Diagnostic ($110) uses the BodyStat 1500 MDD, a medical-grade bioimpedance device, to establish your body composition baseline: fat mass, lean mass, and hydration markers. Combined with a 9-point movement screen, it gives us objective data to build your program from and to measure real progress against at rescans. If you continue into a training package, the diagnostic fee is credited in full toward a 3-month package.

What certifications does your trainer hold?

Nemezio LopezPerez holds NASM-CPT, NASM-CES (Corrective Exercise Specialist), NASM-PES (Performance Enhancement Specialist), NASM-BCS (Behavior Change Specialist), NASM-CNC (Certified Nutrition Coach), and the ACE Corrective Exercise Specialist credential, plus Precision Nutrition certifications. All programming follows evidence-based, assessment-driven protocols.

I travel frequently for work. Can I still train consistently?

Absolutely. This is one of the most common needs among Del Mar clients. Our Hybrid & Virtual Coaching combines in-person sessions when you're home with virtual programming and hotel-friendly workouts when you're traveling. You stay on track regardless of your schedule.

What if I have chronic pain or a previous injury?

Our corrective-first methodology is designed exactly for this. Every program begins with a comprehensive movement assessment that maps pain patterns, compensations, and restrictions. We follow a strict 3/10 pain ceiling protocol, building around your limitations, not through them. Learn more about our corrective exercise programming.
