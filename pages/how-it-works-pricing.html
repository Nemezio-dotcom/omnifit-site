The Process

What Happens From Day One

Every OmniFit program follows a structured path designed to build results that last. Here's exactly what to expect:

1

Paid Consultation (45 min)

We start with a conversation, not a sales pitch. A refundable $30 deposit reserves your time. You'll share your goals, history, schedule constraints, and past experiences. We'll be honest about whether OmniFit is the right fit. If it's not, we'll tell you and recommend alternatives.

2

Comprehensive Assessment

A 45-minute movement and lifestyle screen: joint function, posture analysis, pain history, mobility tests, and schedule mapping. This establishes your baseline metrics across all seven tracked benchmarks and shapes your entire individualized program. No workout happens until your coach fully understands your body.

3

Custom Program Build

Your coach designs a fully individualized plan, not a template. Programming addresses your specific joint limitations, corrective needs, strength goals, and schedule reality. Delivered through our coaching app with full exercise video library and nutrition targets.

4

Training Begins (Correct → Build → Sustain)

Sessions follow the OmniFit methodology: corrective work first, then progressive strength, then sustainable independence. You'll receive weekly check-ins, nutrition accountability, and a formal 30-day progress report tracking all seven metrics against your baseline.

5

Monthly Reviews & Program Updates

Every 30 days, we review your data, adjust your program, and set targets for the next phase. No more repeating the same routine for months. Every adjustment is based on measurable trends, not opinions.

Policies: No Fine Print

Our Policies: Transparent and Direct

We believe policies should be simple enough to explain in one sentence each. Here they are:

Billing

All programs are billed monthly via auto-pay. No large upfront payments required. 3-month commitment clients agree to a 3-month term billed monthly, then continue month-to-month.

Session Cancellation

24-hour notice required. Late cancellations or no-shows count as completed sessions. This protects both your accountability and our schedule.

Schedule Changes

Life happens. Sessions can be rescheduled with 24-hour notice, converted to virtual, or adapted to travel workouts. We build around your reality.

No Hidden Fees

Your rate includes everything: programming, nutrition coaching, app access, check-ins, and progress reviews. No upsells and no surprise charges.

Pausing Your Program

3-month commitment programs include a pause option of up to 2 weeks per term. Your commitment extends by the same duration at no cost. Month-to-month clients can cancel with 30 days notice.

Ending Your Program

Month-to-month: 30 days written notice. 3-month commitment: honored through the term, then month-to-month. No guilt, no hard feelings. We'll give you a transition plan so your progress doesn't disappear.
