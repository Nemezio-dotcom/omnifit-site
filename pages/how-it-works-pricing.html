The Process

What Happens From Day One

Every OmniFit program follows a structured path designed to build results that last. Here's exactly what to expect:

1

Paid Consultation (45 min)

We start with a conversation, not a sales pitch. A $30 deposit, refunded the same day you attend your consultation, reserves your time. You'll share your goals, history, schedule constraints, and past experiences. We'll be honest about whether OmniFit is the right fit. If it's not, we'll tell you and recommend alternatives.

2

Comprehensive Assessment

If the consultation shows it's a fit, the in-person Performance Assessment measures where you start: the 9-point movement screen, Bodystat QuadScan 4000 body composition, blood pressure, SpO2, grip strength, posture photos and circumference measurements. It sets your baselines for the 12-metric progress framework (12 metrics, measured and self-reported, in 7 categories) and shapes your entire individualized program. No workout happens until your coach fully understands your body.

3

Custom Program Build

Your coach designs a fully individualized plan, not a template. Programming addresses your specific joint limitations, corrective needs, strength goals, and schedule reality. Delivered through our coaching app with full exercise video library and nutrition targets. You then enrol: new clients start on The Foundation, a defined 12-week starter block, and experienced clients can start on The Program directly.

4

Training Begins (Correct → Build → Sustain)

Sessions follow the OmniFit methodology: corrective work first, then progressive strength, then sustainable independence. You'll receive weekly check-ins, nutrition accountability, and a progress review every 4 weeks (at least every 12 weeks on Train Only) against your baseline.

5

Progress Reviews & Program Updates

Every 4 weeks (at least every 12 weeks on Train Only), we review your data, adjust your program, and set targets for the next phase. No more repeating the same routine for months. Every adjustment is based on measurable trends, not opinions.

6

Graduation (the Tune-Up)

As you need less coaching, your Accountability Dial steps down: Full Accountability → Coached → Train Only → Tune-Up. The Tune-Up is the graduation point: one reassessment on the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 and an hour of training every 4 weeks, with your program in the app between visits.

Policies: No Fine Print

Our Policies: Transparent and Direct

We believe policies should be simple enough to explain in one sentence each. Here they are:

Billing

The Foundation is paid in full at enrolment. The Program is billed every 4 weeks via auto-pay and continues until cancelled.

Training may be HSA or FSA eligible with a Letter of Medical Necessity from a licensed provider. See how HSA and FSA eligibility works.

Session Cancellation

24-hour notice required. Late cancellations or no-shows count as completed sessions. This protects both your accountability and our schedule.

Schedule Changes

Life happens. Sessions can be rescheduled with 24-hour notice or adapted to travel workouts, and on the Travel Protocol you can take a scheduled session live by video during a travel week, at no extra cost. We build around your reality.

No Hidden Fees

Your rate includes everything: programming, nutrition coaching, app access, check-ins, and progress reviews. No upsells and no surprise charges.

Pausing Your Program

The Foundation includes two pause weeks across the course with notice, extending the end date. The Program includes six pause weeks per twelve months with two weeks' notice, credited against the next cycle.

Ending Your Program

The Program: 30 days' written notice, with remaining credit applied to the final cycle. The Foundation runs to the end of its three cycles. No guilt, no hard feelings. We'll give you a transition plan so your progress doesn't disappear.
