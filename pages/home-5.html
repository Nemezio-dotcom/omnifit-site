Meet Nemezio LopezPerez

Nemezio LopezPerez founded OmniFit Performance after his own athletic years ended in stacked injuries and a rebuild that took years. Working around what his joints would tolerate, instead of arguing with them, taught him the principle the practice runs on: strength training should make your body more durable, not less.

Today Coach Nemezio trains clients at Teqneeq Functional Health Center in 4S Ranch, in their homes across North County, and by live video. Most are professionals over 30 who run the rest of their lives on structure and data and want the same rigor in their training. He measures first, corrects before loading, and counts it a success when a client needs him less.

Frequently Asked Questions

+Is OmniFit too advanced if I've never strength trained?
No. Never having trained is a common starting point here. The Performance Assessment shows where you are, corrective work comes before any load, and every exercise stays under a 3/10 pain ceiling, so the program starts at your level. Ken, a CTO in his 50s, had never followed a structured program before OmniFit; his story is on the case studies page.
+Is OmniFit worth the price compared to a regular gym membership?
Yes, if you want a plan, not just access: OmniFit builds your program from your assessment. The $150 Performance Assessment is credited toward your first cycle. If OmniFit isn't the right fit within your first 30 days on The Foundation, The Program or the Executive Reset, tell us in writing and we'll refund the unused portion of your enrollment. Pause weeks, credit for sessions OmniFit cancels and The Foundation's three 4-week cycles in one payment protect your progress.
+What happens when my program ends?
You graduate. Your Accountability Dial steps down as you need less coaching, Full Accountability → Coached → Train Only → Tune-Up, and the Tune-Up is the graduation point: one reassessment on the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 and an hour of training every 4 weeks, with your program in the app between visits. The goal is a client who no longer needs us every week.
+Do you work with people over 50?
Yes. Active adults 50+ are a core part of the practice. Training starts from your assessment, corrective work is part of every warm-up, loading stays under a 3/10 pain ceiling, and reassessment happens at least every 12 weeks so the program keeps pace with how you recover. See strength training for adults over 50 for the details.
+What if I travel a lot for work?
The Travel Protocol covers it, for studio and in-home clients. You get travel-adapted programming in the app for hotel gyms and rooms, check-ins that continue at your Accountability Dial setting, and pause weeks when a trip can't hold training. You can take a scheduled session live by video during a travel week, at no extra cost. Fully remote clients use the 30-Minute Executive Reset.
+Do you offer virtual personal training in San Diego?
Yes, and nationwide. Fully remote clients train through the 30-Minute Executive Reset: live video sessions with Coach Nemezio on most tiers, your program and check-ins in the OmniFit app, and minimal gear such as a pair of dumbbells and a mat. Sessions fit between meetings and work from a home, an office or a hotel room.
+Is nutrition coaching included?
Yes, in every program, with no add-ons. Your coach sets calorie and protein targets and simple meal frameworks that fit your schedule, work dinners and travel included. How closely nutrition is followed depends on your Accountability Dial setting: daily logging at Full Accountability, weekly check-ins at Coached, and on request at Train Only.
