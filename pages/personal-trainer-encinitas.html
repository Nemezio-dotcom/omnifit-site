Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every Encinitas client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your program starts with a $110 Performance Diagnostic and a comprehensive movement screening: overhead squat assessment, posture documentation, joint range of motion testing, and nine total benchmarks.

Encinitas has a wellness-oriented culture, and many residents are already active: yoga, surfing, hiking, cycling. That's an advantage, but it also creates blind spots. Repetitive movement patterns build strength in some planes while leaving others weak. Hip flexors shorten from cycling. Shoulders develop instability from overhead yoga poses done with poor scapular control. Surf paddling creates thoracic imbalances.

Our corrective-first approach doesn't replace what you love doing. It strengthens the foundation that makes it sustainable. We fix the imbalances your activities create, then build strength where it matters most.

What is the Performance Diagnostic?

The OmniFit Performance Diagnostic ($110) uses the BodyStat 1500 MDD, a medical-grade bioimpedance device, to establish your body composition baseline: fat mass, lean mass, and hydration markers. Combined with a 9-point movement screen, it gives us objective data to build your program from and to measure real progress against at rescans. If you continue into a training package, the diagnostic fee is credited in full toward a 3-month package.

I practice yoga regularly. How does strength training fit with that?

Yoga builds flexibility and body awareness, but it doesn't build the progressive resistance loading your muscles and bones need for long-term health, especially after 30. Our programming complements yoga by adding the corrective stability and strength work that yoga alone can't provide. We won't compromise your mobility. We'll enhance it by addressing the imbalances that even consistent yoga practice creates.

I've had a shoulder injury that flares up with certain movements. Can you work around that?

We don't just work around it. We address it. Our corrective-first approach begins with a comprehensive assessment that identifies exactly what's causing the flare-up. We follow a strict 3/10 pain ceiling protocol, systematically restoring shoulder function through inhibition, lengthening, activation, and integration before loading it progressively.
