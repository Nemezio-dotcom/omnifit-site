Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every Carmel Valley client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your program starts with a $110 Performance Diagnostic and a comprehensive movement screening, not a workout. We identify exactly what your body needs before programming a single exercise.

Carmel Valley's tech and biotech professionals are some of the most driven people we work with, but that drive often means years of ignoring physical warning signs. Tight hips from 10-hour desk days. Rounded shoulders from laptop posture. Lower back stiffness that gets managed with ibuprofen instead of corrective work.

Our corrective-first approach breaks that cycle. We fix the root cause, then build strength on a stable foundation. The result is training that actually lasts, not short-term motivation that fades after a few weeks.

The Diagnostic

The 9-Point Movement Screen

Every OmniFit program starts with a 45-minute consultation and the $110 Performance Diagnostic, credited in full toward a 3-month package. The diagnostic includes a nine-point movement screen:

  • Overhead Squat Assessment — compensations in ankles, knees, hips, and shoulders under a loaded pattern
  • Single-Leg Squat — unilateral stability, hip strength, knee tracking
  • Push & Pull Patterns — shoulder mechanics and scapular stability under load
  • Posture Photos — front, side, and back baseline, re-scored quarterly
  • Joint Range of Motion — shoulders, hips, ankles, thoracic spine
  • Grip Strength — a reliable marker of functional strength, tracked monthly
  • BodyStat Body Composition — medical-grade bioimpedance via the BodyStat 1500 MDD: lean mass, fat mass, hydration
  • Core Stability — plank and anti-rotation testing
  • Gait Analysis — walking mechanics that reveal asymmetries and compensations

Every finding feeds directly into your program. Nothing is generic, and nothing is guessed.
