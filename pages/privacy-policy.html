Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to OmniFit Performance ("OmniFit," "we," "our," or "us"). Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit www.omnifittraining.com or use our services online or in person.

01

Who We Are

OmniFit operates under OmniFit Performance LLC (formerly JJ4E LLC), based in San Diego, California (4S Ranch). We provide personal training, nutritional coaching, and online fitness programs for professionals and clients nationwide.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact or work with us:

  • Name, phone number, and email address
  • Demographic information such as location or interests
  • Fitness, health, and lifestyle details that you choose to share
  • Payment and billing information (processed securely through Stripe, Klarna, or Affirm)
  • Website usage data through Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity
03

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide personalized training and coaching services
  • Respond to inquiries, schedule consultations, and manage accounts
  • Process payments and maintain records
  • Improve our website, content, and client experience

04

Data Storage and Security

We keep your data secure with encryption and password protection. Trainer data is managed through Trainerize, which follows international data protection standards. We never sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

05

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies to understand how visitors use the site and improve performance. Cookies may track pages visited, time spent on site, and browser type. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. We also use Google Analytics 4 for anonymous traffic and usage data, and Microsoft Clarity for session analytics with form fields masked; Clarity is not used on the Fit Check.

06

Sharing Information

We only share data with trusted partners who help us operate our business, such as:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, Klarna, Affirm)
  • Booking (Calendly)
  • Coaching platforms (Trainerize)

  • Hosting (Cloudflare)

These partners are required to protect your data and use it only for agreed purposes.
