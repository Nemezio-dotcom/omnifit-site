The Problem

Why Most Fat Loss Programs Fail Professionals Over 30

Most fat loss programs are designed for people with unlimited time, no injuries, and simple schedules. They rely on extreme restriction and high-volume exercise to produce fast results that don't last. For professionals over 30 dealing with desk jobs, travel, old injuries, stress, and poor sleep — these approaches don't just fail, they make things worse.

Bootcamps Cause Injuries

Questions

Fat Loss FAQs

How much weight can I realistically lose?
The rate depends on your starting point, adherence, stress, sleep, and metabolic factors — all of which are assessed and managed throughout the program. OmniFit Performance does not promise a rate of weight loss, because crash approaches lead to rebound weight gain. Progress is measured against your own baseline across twelve metrics.
How is this different from F45, Orangetheory, or a 6-week challenge?
Those are group classes, and they suit people who want a class format. OmniFit is private 1-on-1 coaching with individualized training, personalized nutrition targets, corrective exercise, and behavior change coaching. The goal is permanent transformation, not a temporary number on the scale.
Will I keep the weight off?
Long-term maintenance is built into the program. The Sustain phase focuses on skills transfer — by the time coaching ends, you'll understand your nutrition targets, know how to structure your own training, and have built sustainable habits around sleep and stress.
Do I need to follow a strict diet?
No. We use a flexible nutrition framework based on calorie and protein targets with simple meal structures — not rigid meal plans or food elimination. Designed for professionals who eat out, travel, and have unpredictable schedules.
Is this safe for someone with joint issues or old injuries?
Yes. Every program begins with a movement assessment, and all training follows the 3/10 pain ceiling rule. Exercises are selected and modified based on your joint health. High-impact movements are replaced with equally effective alternatives.
Can I do this virtually if I travel?
Yes. The Executive Reset delivers live virtual HIIT and strength sessions with full nutrition coaching and accountability. Many clients combine in-person and virtual depending on their schedule, and studio and in-home clients on the Travel Protocol can take a scheduled session live by video during a travel week, at no extra cost.
How do you track progress beyond the scale?
We track weekly weight trends (averages, not daily fluctuations), body measurements every 4 weeks, progress photos, strength benchmarks, and adherence metrics. This multi-metric approach helps show whether the weight you lose is fat or muscle, rather than guessing. See our full tracking system.
What does it cost?
OmniFit is premium coaching, not a gym membership. Programs bill in 4-week cycles, with every rate published. See current rates or book a consultation to discuss which program fits your goals and budget.
Can I lose 20–30 pounds without crash dieting?
That is who this program is built for. Instead of severe restriction, your coach sets calorie and protein targets and simple meal frameworks that fit your schedule, paired with joint-friendly strength training to protect muscle while you lose fat. OmniFit doesn't promise a rate of loss: progress is measured against your own baseline, so the pace stays one you can live with.
What happens to my results after the program?
You keep the skills that produced them, and coaching steps down as you need less of it: Full Accountability → Coached → Train Only → Tune-Up. The Tune-Up is the graduation point: one reassessment on the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 and an hour of training every 4 weeks, with your program in the app between visits, so any drift shows up in the numbers early.
