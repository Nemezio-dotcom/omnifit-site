Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every Rancho Bernardo client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your program starts with a $150 Performance Assessment and a comprehensive movement screening that maps compensations, restrictions, and asymmetries before we design a single exercise.

Rancho Bernardo residents tend to be active: surfing, cycling, running on the bluffs. That's a strength, but it also means accumulated wear patterns that go unaddressed. Tight hip flexors from cycling. Shoulder impingement from paddling. Knee strain from running on uneven terrain. Our corrective-first approach identifies and resolves these patterns before layering strength work on top.

The result: you keep doing what you love, but with a body that moves better, recovers faster, and resists injury.

The Diagnostic

The 9-Point Movement Screen

Every OmniFit program starts with a 45-minute consultation and the $150 Performance Assessment, credited toward your first cycle. The diagnostic includes a nine-point movement screen:

  • Overhead Squat Assessment — compensations in ankles, knees, hips, and shoulders under a loaded pattern
  • Single-Leg Squat — unilateral stability, hip strength, knee tracking
  • Push & Pull Patterns — shoulder mechanics and scapular stability under load
  • Posture Photos — front, side, and back baseline, re-scored quarterly
  • Joint Range of Motion — shoulders, hips, ankles, thoracic spine
  • Grip Strength — a reliable marker of functional strength, tracked monthly
  • Bodystat Body Composition — medical-grade bioimpedance via the Bodystat QuadScan 4000: lean mass, fat mass, hydration
  • Core Stability — plank and anti-rotation testing
  • Gait Analysis — walking mechanics that reveal asymmetries and compensations

Every finding feeds directly into your program. Nothing is generic, and nothing is guessed.

I surf and cycle regularly. Will strength training complement that?

Absolutely, and this is where corrective-first programming is especially valuable. Surfing and cycling create specific muscle imbalances (tight hip flexors, rounded shoulders, limited thoracic rotation) that eventually lead to pain and injury if left unaddressed. We build your strength program around your recreational activities, correcting the wear patterns they create while building the stability and power that makes them more enjoyable.
