Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every La Jolla client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your program starts with a $150 Performance Assessment and a comprehensive movement screening that maps your body's compensations, restrictions, and asymmetries: objective data before a single rep is programmed.

We address dysfunction before adding load. This is the phase most trainers skip, and it's the reason most professionals get injured or plateau. Whether you're dealing with desk-related posture issues, recovering from surgery, or returning to training after years away, the method adapts to your body.

La Jolla clients especially benefit from the in-home format: no commute time, sessions that fit between meetings, and training in a comfortable, private environment. We bring the expertise. You just show up.

Who We Coach

Who We Coach

The professionals we coach in La Jolla tend to arrive in one of three patterns:

The Desk-Bound Executive

Ten-hour days at a desk, rounded shoulders, a forward head, and an old lifting or sports injury that flares up whenever training gets serious. The program starts corrective: fix the movement patterns first, then build strength on top of them.

The Post-Rehab Professional

Discharged from physical therapy, cleared to train, but nobody showed them how to load safely again. OmniFit is the bridge: a strict 3/10 pain ceiling rule, progressive strength work, and coordination with physical therapists when needed.

The 50+ Professional

Done with bootcamps their joints will not tolerate, but not done getting stronger. Age-appropriate progressive strength training, calibrated to recovery capacity, measured against their own baseline.

The Diagnostic

The 9-Point Movement Screen

Every OmniFit program starts with a 45-minute consultation and the $150 Performance Assessment, credited toward your first cycle. The diagnostic includes a nine-point movement screen:

  • Overhead Squat Assessment — compensations in ankles, knees, hips, and shoulders under a loaded pattern
  • Single-Leg Squat — unilateral stability, hip strength, knee tracking
  • Push & Pull Patterns — shoulder mechanics and scapular stability under load
  • Posture Photos — front, side, and back baseline, re-scored quarterly
  • Joint Range of Motion — shoulders, hips, ankles, thoracic spine
  • Grip Strength — a reliable marker of functional strength, tracked monthly
  • Bodystat Body Composition — medical-grade bioimpedance via the Bodystat QuadScan 4000: lean mass, fat mass, hydration
  • Core Stability — plank and anti-rotation testing
  • Gait Analysis — walking mechanics that reveal asymmetries and compensations

Every finding feeds directly into your program. Nothing is generic, and nothing is guessed.

Questions About Training in La Jolla

How does in-home personal training work in La Jolla?

We bring all necessary equipment to your La Jolla home: adjustable dumbbells, resistance bands, suspension trainers, and mats. Sessions are 45–60 minutes, fully programmed in advance based on your assessment data. All you need is about 8×8 feet of clear space: garage, patio, living room, or backyard all work. A per-session travel fee may apply depending on location.

What does a personal trainer in La Jolla cost?

OmniFit Performance publishes its rates in full. New clients start on The Foundation: three consecutive 4-week cycles paid in full at enrolment, $560 per cycle for studio Momentum, $1,100 for studio Performance, $800 for in-home Momentum, $850 for studio couples and $1,250 for in-home couples. After that, or by direct entry, The Program bills every 4 weeks: $640 to $1,800 at the studio, $900 to $2,600 in-home, $1,000 to $2,900 for couples. Without a program, single sessions are $205 to $275 and 5-packs run $180 to $250 per session depending on setting and membership. The fully virtual Executive Reset runs $200 to $1,100 per 4 weeks. Every client starts with a $150 Performance Assessment, credited toward the first cycle. Note: studio programs require a Teqneeq membership ($119 per month, billed by Teqneeq), with facility access on non-training days included. Full tables on the training rates page.

Do you train only in La Jolla?

No. La Jolla marks the southern edge of our concierge in-home territory. We also serve Del Mar, Solana Beach, Encinitas, Carlsbad, Carmel Valley, Rancho Santa Fe, and 4S Ranch. Studio sessions are available inside Teqneeq FHC, a members-only functional health and performance lab at 10772 Thornmint Rd in 4S Ranch, San Diego, about 30 minutes from La Jolla.

What is the Performance Assessment?

The OmniFit Performance Assessment ($150) uses the Bodystat QuadScan 4000, a medical-grade bioimpedance device, to establish your body composition baseline: fat mass, lean mass, and hydration markers. Combined with a 9-point movement screen, it gives us objective data to build your program from and to measure real progress against at rescans. If you continue into a training package, the diagnostic fee is credited toward your first cycle.

What certifications does your trainer hold?

OmniFit's founder Nemezio LopezPerez holds NASM-CPT (Certified Personal Trainer), NASM-CES (Corrective Exercise Specialist), NASM-PES (Performance Enhancement Specialist), NASM-BCS (Behavior Change Specialist), NASM-CNC (Certified Nutrition Coach), and the ACE Corrective Exercise Specialist credential, plus Precision Nutrition certifications. All programming is evidence-based and assessment-driven.

How is OmniFit different from a big-box gym or boutique fitness studio?

OmniFit is fully individualized, one-on-one training, not group workouts. Every session is programmed for your specific body, goals, and assessment data. There are no templates, no shared attention, and no competition element. See our full comparison with F45, Orangetheory, and big-box gyms.

What if I have chronic pain or a past injury?

This is exactly what our corrective-first approach is designed for. We begin every program with a comprehensive movement assessment that identifies pain patterns, compensations, and restrictions. We follow a strict 3/10 pain ceiling protocol and build programming around your limitations, not in spite of them. Learn more about our corrective exercise and post-rehab programming.
