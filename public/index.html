Who It's For

Is a Personal Trainer in North County San Diego Right for You?

Most people who come to OmniFit are in one of three situations.

Progress has stalled despite the effort

You train, you try, and the numbers stopped moving. We measure where you actually are, find what is holding the plan back, and build from that baseline instead of guessing.

Joints, old injuries, or just finishing PT

Corrective work comes first and stays in every warm-up, with loading kept under a 3/10 pain ceiling.

Physical therapy treats; OmniFit trains. We pick up where care ends, coordinate with your clinician when you allow it, and never diagnose or treat.

A calendar or travel that wrecks routines

Your program lives in the app between sessions, pause weeks absorb the busy stretches, and the Travel Protocol keeps you training on the road.

Never trained before, or over 50? You are welcome here. The corrective-first approach exists for exactly that. Even if you just want to train, we can start there.

Not the right fit if…

  • You are looking for the cheapest option. Every session is 1:1 and measured, and the rates reflect that coaching time.
  • You want classes or a social gym. There are no group sessions here, so a class studio will suit you better.
  • You are not ready to show up and track. Booked sessions and check-ins are the standards that protect your slot and your results.

How It Works

How Our Executive Fitness Coaching Works

Four phases, in the same order for every client. What changes is what your assessment finds.

  1. 1

    Assess

    A 45-minute consultation, then the Performance Assessment: nine movement benchmarks, posture photos, strength baselines, circumference measurements and Bodystat QuadScan 4000 body composition.

  2. 2

    Correct

    Corrective work addresses what the assessment found before load goes up, under a 3/10 pain ceiling.

  3. 3

    Build

    Progressive strength in 4-week blocks, with nutrition and habits coached alongside and progress judged against your own baseline.

  4. 4

    Sustain

    Coaching steps down one setting at a time until you train on your own, with a Tune-Up to keep you on track.

OmniFit runs on the OmniFit Method: Measured Progress, Joint-First Strength and Accountability That Travels, carried through Assess → Correct → Build → Sustain.

We teach you to graduate from us

Your Accountability Dial steps down as you need less of us: Full Accountability → Coached → Train Only → Tune-Up. The Tune-Up is graduation, not another setting.

Tracking is a diagnostic tool, not a life sentence. We measure closely at the start so we can coach less over time. The goal is a client who no longer needs us every week.

See the full OmniFit Method

  1. Full AccountabilityDaily logging and a coach reply within 1 business day.
  2. CoachedA weekly written check-in and 1-3 habit targets.
  3. Train OnlySessions plus your app program, with a one-tap weekly check-in.
  4. Tune-UpGraduation: one reassessment and an hour of training every 4 weeks.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

+Is OmniFit too advanced if I've never strength trained?
No. Never having trained is a common starting point here. The Performance Assessment shows where you are, corrective work comes before any load, and every exercise stays under a 3/10 pain ceiling, so the program starts at your level. Ken, a CTO in his 50s, had never followed a structured program before OmniFit; his story is on the case studies page.
+Is OmniFit worth the price compared to a regular gym membership?
Yes, if you want a plan, not just access: OmniFit builds your program from your assessment. The Performance Assessment ($150 in person, $75 virtual) is credited toward your first cycle. If OmniFit isn't the right fit within your first 30 days on The Foundation, The Program or the Executive Reset, tell us in writing and we'll refund the unused portion of your enrollment. Pause weeks, credit for sessions OmniFit cancels and The Foundation's three 4-week cycles in one payment protect your progress.
+What happens when my program ends?
You graduate. Your Accountability Dial steps down as you need less coaching, Full Accountability → Coached → Train Only → Tune-Up, and the Tune-Up is the graduation point: one reassessment on the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 and an hour of training every 4 weeks, with your program in the app between visits. The goal is a client who no longer needs us every week.
+Do you work with people over 50?
Yes. Active adults 50+ are a core part of the practice. Training starts from your assessment, corrective work is part of every warm-up, loading stays under a 3/10 pain ceiling, and reassessment happens at least every 12 weeks so the program keeps pace with how you recover. See strength training for adults over 50 for the details.
+What if I travel a lot for work?
The Travel Protocol covers it, for studio and in-home clients. You get travel-adapted programming in the app for hotel gyms and rooms, check-ins that continue at your Accountability Dial setting, and pause weeks when a trip can't hold training. You can take a scheduled session live by video during a travel week, at no extra cost. Fully remote clients use the 30-Minute Executive Reset.
+Do you offer virtual personal training in San Diego?
Yes, and nationwide. Fully remote clients train through the 30-Minute Executive Reset: live video sessions with Coach Nemezio on most tiers, your program and check-ins in the OmniFit app, and minimal gear such as a pair of dumbbells and a mat. Sessions fit between meetings and work from a home, an office or a hotel room.
+Is nutrition coaching included?
Yes, in every program, with no add-ons. Your coach sets calorie and protein targets and simple meal frameworks that fit your schedule, work dinners and travel included. How closely nutrition is followed depends on your Accountability Dial setting: daily logging at Full Accountability, weekly check-ins at Coached, and on request at Train Only.
