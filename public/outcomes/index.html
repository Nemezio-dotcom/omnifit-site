Method

How These Figures Were Calculated

Every figure comes from OmniFit's own client records, pulled on October 1, 2026. No client who met the eligibility criteria for a figure was removed because of their outcome.

  • Sources. Session schedules and workout logs from the OmniFit coaching app (Trainerize); payment history from Stripe.
  • Who is counted. Anyone with two or more scheduled sessions or logged workouts. Family members billed to one card are counted separately when each has their own training record.
  • Sessions. Scheduled sessions. Attendance was not recorded before October 2026, so a small number may have been cancelled or missed.
  • Training duration. Days between a client's first and last session or logged workout. A milestone rate counts only clients whose first session was at least that long before October 1, 2026. It reflects recorded training activity, not a contract term.
  • Strength. For each lift logged at least four times over at least four weeks, estimated one-rep max from logged weight and reps (Epley formula, sets of 12 reps or fewer). Change compares the best of the latest two sessions with the best of the first two. Each client's result is the median across their lifts. Best sustained level is the highest average of any two consecutive sessions on a lift after the first two. Bodyweight exercises such as pull-ups are excluded.
  • Working load. Heaviest logged weight on each lift in the first session versus the heaviest between weeks 10 and 16.
  • Weight. First and latest logged body weight at least four weeks apart, from in-session scales and clients' own synced scales.
  • Body composition. Bodystat QuadScan 4000 bioimpedance readings taken in session at Teqneeq Functional Health Center, available only for clients who started training there.
  • Goal achievement. Coach assessments against the goal set at intake, not client-reported results. Client-confirmed ratings begin with clients starting in October 2026.
  • Limits. There is no comparison group, so these figures describe what OmniFit observed, not how much of the change OmniFit caused versus what clients would have achieved otherwise. Sample sizes vary by measure and are stated beside each figure; the body-composition group is small.
