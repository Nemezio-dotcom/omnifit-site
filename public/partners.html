Clinical Partners Network

The Practitioners Behind the Performance

OmniFit Performance partners with select board-certified clinicians and licensed therapists in San Diego to bridge the gap between rehabilitation, recovery, and long-term corrective strength training. Every referral is earned by outcomes, not affiliations.

Our Referral Philosophy

We don't partner with everyone. OmniFit's clinical partners are licensed professionals who share our commitment to evidence-based corrective work, measurable outcomes, and the belief that no one should be left in the gap between "cleared by PT" and "ready to train at full capacity." Our clients deserve practitioners who understand executive lifestyles, travel demands, and the value of their time.

The PT-to-Performance Bridge

Why Clinical Partnerships Matter at OmniFit

OmniFit Performance in San Diego maintains a referral partnership with a board-certified orthopedic physical therapist and a licensed massage therapist to ensure a seamless transition from rehabilitation and recovery to corrective strength training. Founding clinical partner Dr. Damien Jackson-Ricketts, DPT, OCS, operates Stoke Physical Therapy — a concierge practice serving active professionals and athletes. Recovery partner Celeste Esposito, LMT, operates Serenity Massage, providing therapeutic bodywork that addresses the soft tissue restrictions corrective exercise alone cannot resolve.

This "PT-to-Performance Bridge" model means post-rehab clients are never left without structured guidance after being cleared by their clinician — and clients with chronic tension, adhesions, or recovery demands have a direct pathway to targeted soft tissue therapy coordinated with their training program. Each partnership is built on referrals in both directions and, with your written permission, shared notes, so your training fits around the care your clinician provides. OmniFit's NASM Corrective Exercise Specialist (CES) methodology is designed to pick up where clinical care ends: OmniFit trains, and the clinicians treat.

Use Cases

What People Come to Coaching For

Coaching is rarely the goal on its own. These are the situations it usually attaches to.

Long-Term Transformation

You want a body that holds up for years rather than a twelve-week push that unravels. The work is built to be repeatable: a plan you can still follow in month nine, progressions tied to what your body tolerates, and a coach who adjusts the plan rather than restarting it.

Fat Loss Without Crash Protocols

Aggressive plans usually fail on the joints or the schedule before they fail on willpower. Training your body tolerates is what makes consistency possible, and nutrition is coached alongside it with individualized macros rather than a template handed over on day one.

Desk-Worker Posture and Mobility

Long sitting hours, stiffness that arrives before the workday ends, and the sense of moving worse than you used to. Programming targets the specific patterns desk work creates, with corrective work built into every session rather than bolted on as a warm-up.

Data-Driven Hybrid Coaching

You travel, your calendar moves, and you want the work to continue anyway. In-person sessions combine with app-delivered programming, the Travel Protocol covers the weeks you're on the road, and the same benchmarks are tracked either way so nothing is guessed between visits.

Ready to Train With Clinical Confidence?

Whether you're finishing physical therapy, need coordinated soft tissue work, or want a trainer who communicates with your care team, OmniFit's referral partners help keep your training and your care coordinated, with your permission.
