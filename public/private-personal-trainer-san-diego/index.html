What "Private" Actually Means

This Isn't a Gym With a Trainer. It's a Different Model.

Most "personal training" in San Diego means a trainer who splits attention with the gym floor around them: other clients, waiting for equipment, music you didn't choose. OmniFit operates differently: dedicated 1:1 coaching inside a credentialed, members-only facility.

✗

Commercial Gym + Trainer

Crowded floor, shared equipment, background noise. Your trainer's attention is split between you and the chaos around you. Generic programs, minimal assessment.

✗

Group & Bootcamp Studios

One coach, many clients, one workout for everyone. No individual assessment, no corrective work, no program built for your body or your injury history.

✓

One client, one coach, full attention, inside Teqneeq FHC, a credentialed-only performance lab. No group classes, no crowds. A program built entirely around your assessment.

Use Cases

What People Come to Private Coaching For

Private coaching is rarely the goal on its own. These are the situations it usually attaches to.

Long-Term Transformation

You want a body that holds up for years rather than a twelve-week push that unravels. The work is built to be repeatable: a plan you can still follow in month nine, progressions tied to what your body tolerates, and a coach who adjusts the plan rather than restarting it.

Fat Loss Without Crash Protocols

Aggressive plans usually fail on the joints or the schedule before they fail on willpower. Training your body tolerates is what makes consistency possible, and nutrition is coached alongside it with individualized macros rather than a template handed over on day one.

Desk-Worker Posture and Mobility

Long sitting hours, stiffness that arrives before the workday ends, and the sense of moving worse than you used to. Programming targets the specific patterns desk work creates, with corrective work built into every session rather than bolted on as a warm-up.

Data-Driven Hybrid Coaching

You travel, your calendar moves, and you want the work to continue anyway. In-person sessions combine with app-delivered programming, and the same benchmarks are tracked either way so nothing is guessed between visits.

How much does private personal training cost at OmniFit?
Every program begins with the $150 OmniFit Performance Assessment, a Bodystat body composition baseline plus movement screen, credited toward your first cycle. In-studio private training is offered as three named tiers by frequency: Momentum, Performance, and Peak. On The Program they run $640, $1,250, and $1,800 per 4 weeks. Without a program, 5-packs run $180 to $250 per session and single sessions are $205 for studio members, $235 for non-members, or $275 in-home. The fully virtual Executive Reset starts at $200 per 4 weeks for the async tier. There are no activation fees. Full details on the Training Rates page.
What is the Performance Assessment?
The OmniFit Performance Assessment ($150) uses the Bodystat QuadScan 4000, a medical-grade bioimpedance device, to establish your body composition baseline: fat mass, lean mass, and hydration markers. Combined with a 9-point movement screen, it gives us objective data to build your program from and to measure real progress against at rescans. If you continue into a training package, the assessment fee is credited toward your program.
