Frequently Asked

Questions About Programs

How much does personal training cost in San Diego?

OmniFit Performance offers structured programs billed every 4 weeks, starting at $640 for in-studio training at Teqneeq FHC and $900 for in-home training, with a discounted three-cycle Foundation for new clients from $560 per cycle. Every program includes monthly clinical-grade body composition assessments, custom programming, nutrition guidance, and weekly accountability.

What's included in OmniFit Performance programs?

Every program includes a monthly OmniFit Performance Assessment with clinical-grade Bodystat diagnostics, custom programming based on the OmniFit 4-phase methodology (Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain), nutrition guidance and macro framework, full app access for workout tracking, weekly accountability check-ins, and direct trainer access. In-home programs include all equipment — no home gym required.

How does billing work?

New clients start on The Foundation: three consecutive 4-week cycles, paid in full at enrolment and discounted for that reason. The Program bills every 4 weeks via auto-pay and can be cancelled with 30 days' written notice, with remaining credit applied to the final cycle. There are no activation fees.

What are the program tiers?

OmniFit Performance offers three named programs: Momentum (1 session per week), Performance (2 sessions per week), and Peak (3 sessions per week). Each is available for both Teqneeq FHC in-studio and in-home training, individually or as a couple. New clients start on The Foundation, three consecutive 4-week cycles paid in full at enrolment; after that, or by direct entry with prior training experience, The Program bills every 4 weeks. All tiers include the same comprehensive inclusions — the difference is training frequency.

Do you offer online or virtual personal training?

Yes. The Executive Reset delivers live 1:1 virtual coaching — Gold at $500 per 4 weeks (1 session per week), Platinum at $750 (2 per week), and Black at $1,100 (3 per week). Async-only programming (Bronze) is available at $200 per 4 weeks. Virtual coaching is available nationwide.

What happens after I finish my program?

Graduates of an OmniFit program can continue with the Tune-Up — one session per cycle, a reassessment on the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 and an hour of training, with programming delivered through the OmniFit app between visits. Pricing is $295 per 4 weeks for Teqneeq members, $325 for non-members (day pass included, no facility membership required), or $375 in-home. Billing is every 4 weeks with no long-term commitment; cancel with 30 days notice.

Does OmniFit offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes. Every program includes a 30-Day Fit Guarantee: if after your first 30 days you decide it isn't the right fit, you can cancel and receive a refund of the unused balance of your program. You keep the first month and everything you've learned. There is no compliance test to pass and nothing to negotiate.

What is the OmniFit Performance Assessment?

The OmniFit Performance Assessment is a clinical-grade diagnostic session using the Bodystat QuadScan 4000 for body composition analysis, plus blood pressure, SpO2, movement screening, and grip strength testing. It's available in-studio at Teqneeq FHC or in your home for $150, credited toward your first cycle. Reassessments are included in every program; a client returning after a lapse of 60 days or more books a new assessment. The Bodystat QuadScan 4000 holds CE certification as a Class IIa medical device under MDD 93/42/EEC and has FDA clearance. OmniFit Performance uses it to measure body composition. It is not used to diagnose, treat or assess any medical condition, and OmniFit is not a medical provider.

Can I bring a guest to my training session?

Yes. Clients on any named program (Momentum through Peak) can bring a guest for $90 per session. This is a great way for a spouse, friend, or colleague to experience OmniFit before committing to their own program.

Can I pause my program if I travel?

Yes. The Foundation includes two pause weeks across the course with notice, extending the end date. The Program includes six pause weeks per twelve months with two weeks' notice, credited against the next cycle. Many clients also use the virtual option to continue training during travel instead of pausing.

Where does in-home training take place?

OmniFit Performance serves 4S Ranch, Rancho Santa Fe, Fairbanks Ranch, Santaluz, Carmel Valley, Del Mar, Solana Beach, Encinitas, Carlsbad, La Jolla, Rancho Bernardo, and Poway. All equipment is provided — no home gym required. In-home programs start at $900 per 4 weeks, or $800 per cycle on the three-cycle Foundation.

Program pricing and availability are subject to change. Contact us for the most current rates.
