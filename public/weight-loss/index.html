The Problem

Why Most Fat Loss Programs Fail Professionals Over 30

Most fat loss programs are designed for people with unlimited time, no injuries, and simple schedules. They rely on extreme restriction and high-volume exercise to produce fast results that don't last. For professionals over 30 dealing with desk jobs, travel, old injuries, stress, and poor sleep — these approaches don't just fail, they make things worse.

Crash Diets Backfire
Severe restriction slows metabolism, increases cravings, and leads to rebound weight gain within months.
Bootcamps Cause Injuries
Generic high-impact programming ignores joint health and creates new problems for bodies over 30.
No Accountability = No Consistency
Without weekly check-ins and structured coaching, most professionals drop off within 3–4 weeks.
No Exit Plan
Programs that don't teach you how to eat and train independently leave you needing them forever — or rebounding.
Our Approach

How OmniFit Produces Fat Loss That Lasts

Month 1
Assess & Calibrate

Movement assessment, postural screen, body composition baseline, nutrition audit. We identify where you are, set realistic targets, and build your first phase of programming.

Strength training 2–3x/week, nutrition targets dialed in, weekly check-ins. Body composition begins to shift in this phase while you build strength and improve movement quality.

Month 5–6+

Skills transfer: you learn to manage your own nutrition, training structure, and body awareness. The goal is independence — not lifelong dependence on coaching.

Questions

Fat Loss FAQs

How much weight can I realistically lose?
The rate depends on your starting point, adherence, stress, sleep, and metabolic factors — all of which are assessed and managed throughout the program. OmniFit Performance does not promise a rate of weight loss, because crash approaches lead to rebound weight gain. Progress is measured against your own baseline across twelve metrics.
How is this different from F45, Orangetheory, or a 6-week challenge?
Those are group classes with generic programming designed for fast, unsustainable results. OmniFit is private 1-on-1 coaching with individualized training, personalized nutrition targets, corrective exercise, and behavior change coaching. The goal is permanent transformation, not a temporary number on the scale.
Will I keep the weight off?
Long-term maintenance is built into the program. The Sustain phase focuses on skills transfer — by the time coaching ends, you'll understand your nutrition targets, know how to structure your own training, and have built sustainable habits around sleep and stress.
Do I need to follow a strict diet?
No. We use a flexible nutrition framework based on calorie and protein targets with simple meal structures — not rigid meal plans or food elimination. Designed for professionals who eat out, travel, and have unpredictable schedules.
Is this safe for someone with joint issues or old injuries?
Yes. Every program begins with a movement assessment, and all training follows the 3/10 pain ceiling rule. Exercises are selected and modified based on your joint health. High-impact movements are replaced with equally effective alternatives.
Can I do this virtually if I travel?
Yes. The Executive Reset delivers live virtual HIIT and strength sessions with full nutrition coaching and accountability. Many clients combine in-person and virtual depending on their schedule.
How do you track progress beyond the scale?
We track weekly weight trends (averages, not daily fluctuations), monthly body measurements, progress photos, strength benchmarks, and adherence metrics. This multi-metric approach ensures you're losing fat, not muscle. See our full tracking system.
What does it cost?
OmniFit is premium coaching, not a gym membership. Programs are structured as monthly commitments with transparent pricing. See current rates or book a consultation to discuss which program fits your goals and budget.
