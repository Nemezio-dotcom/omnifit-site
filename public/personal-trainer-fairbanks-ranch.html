Our Approach

The OmniFit Method

Every Fairbanks Ranch client follows the same four-phase methodology: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain. Your engagement begins with a Performance Assessment ($150 in person, $75 virtual) and a comprehensive movement screening: overhead squat assessment, posture documentation, joint range of motion testing, and nine total movement benchmarks.

Fairbanks Ranch clients typically share a profile: successful professionals in their 40s–60s who have invested in home gym equipment but lack the structured programming and expert coaching to use it effectively. Many are managing old injuries: a torn rotator cuff that was never fully rehabbed, chronic lower back stiffness from decades of desk work, or knee pain that limits everyday movement.

Our corrective-first approach addresses these issues systematically before adding training load. The aim: stronger, leaner clients who train without flare-ups under a 3/10 pain ceiling.

The Diagnostic

The 9-Point Movement Screen

Every OmniFit program starts with a 45-minute consultation and the $150 Performance Assessment, credited toward your first cycle. The diagnostic includes a nine-point movement screen:

  • Overhead Squat Assessment — compensations in ankles, knees, hips, and shoulders under a loaded pattern
  • Single-Leg Squat — unilateral stability, hip strength, knee tracking
  • Push & Pull Patterns — shoulder mechanics and scapular stability under load
  • Posture Photos — front, side, and back baseline, re-scored quarterly
  • Joint Range of Motion — shoulders, hips, ankles, thoracic spine
  • Grip Strength — a reliable marker of functional strength, reviewed every 4 weeks
  • Bodystat Body Composition — multi-frequency bioimpedance via the Bodystat QuadScan 4000: lean mass, fat mass, hydration
  • Core Stability — plank and anti-rotation testing
  • Gait Analysis — walking mechanics that reveal asymmetries and compensations

Every finding feeds directly into your program. Nothing is generic, and nothing is guessed.

What does a personal trainer in Fairbanks Ranch cost?

OmniFit Performance publishes its rates in full. New clients start on The Foundation: three consecutive 4-week cycles paid in full at enrollment, $560 per cycle for studio Momentum, $1,100 for studio Performance, $800 for in-home Momentum, $850 for studio couples and $1,250 for in-home couples. After that, or by direct entry, The Program bills every 4 weeks: $640 to $1,800 at the studio, $900 to $2,600+ in-home, $1,000 to $2,900 for couples. Without a program, single sessions are $205 to $275 and 5-packs run $180 to $250 per session depending on setting and membership. The fully virtual Executive Reset runs $200 to $1,100 per 4 weeks. Programs and session packs start with a Performance Assessment ($150 in person, $75 virtual), credited toward your first purchase; for a single session it's recommended, not required. Note: studio programs require a Teqneeq membership ($119 per month, billed by Teqneeq), with facility access on non-training days included. Full tables on the training rates page.

What equipment do I need at home?

None is required. We bring professional-grade portable equipment when needed, so all you need is about 8×8 feet of clear space. That said, most Fairbanks Ranch clients have extensive setups, and we design your program around whatever you have, recommending additions only when they'd genuinely help.

What is the Performance Assessment?

The OmniFit Performance Assessment ($150) uses the Bodystat QuadScan 4000, a multi-frequency bioimpedance device, to establish your body composition baseline: fat mass, lean mass, and hydration markers. Combined with a 9-point movement screen, it gives us objective data to build your program from and to measure real progress against at rescans. The Performance Assessment ($150 in person, $75 virtual) is credited toward your first program, Reset cycle or 5-pack.
