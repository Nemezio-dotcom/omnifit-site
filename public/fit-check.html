Are you the right fit?

Ten questions, about three minutes. You'll get an honest read on fit, the session frequency we'd recommend for your goal, and the options that match your schedule and budget, priced from the published rate card. No answers are saved or sent; only an anonymous count of completed checks and their result is recorded.

Built for you if

  • You want a program written for you, not a template
  • You'd rather know the options and prices before you book
  • You keep your appointments: payment confirms a booking, 24 hours' notice to reschedule

Not the right fit if

  • You mainly want group energy or the lowest price
  • You want a 6-week challenge rather than a plan you keep
  • The booking standards feel like too much

New to training, or just want to work out? That's fine. The Performance Assessment sets your starting point, and you decide how much of the coaching around your sessions you use. Even if you just want to train, we can start there.
