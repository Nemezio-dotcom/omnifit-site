Are you the right fit?

OmniFit is diagnostic-led coaching. We measure, track, and adjust from the numbers. It works for people who are ready, willing, and able to do the daily work. Nine questions, about three minutes. Answer honestly, not the way you think you should. Nothing here is saved or sent, and you'll see your result right away.

Built for you if

  • You want decisions based on data
  • You'll log, measure, and report honestly
  • You've decided, and now is the time
