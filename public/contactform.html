What Happens in Your Consultation
1

Goals & History

We talk about what you want to achieve, what you've tried before, and what your typical week looks like. The more honest you are, the better I can help.

2

Honest Recommendation

I'll tell you what I think your biggest priorities are, which program fits, and what realistic results look like. If we're not the right match, I'll tell you directly.

What happens next: the Performance Assessment

The movement screen happens at your Performance Assessment, not on the consultation call: a look at how you move, where you're compensating, what's tight and what's strong. It tells me more than any questionnaire. In person ($150, studio or home) it's hands-on, so wear comfortable clothes; the virtual assessment ($75) is self-measured with live video guidance and has no body-composition scan.
