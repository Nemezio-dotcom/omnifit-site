The Diagnostic

The 9-Point Movement Screen

Every OmniFit program starts with a 45-minute consultation and the $150 Performance Assessment, credited toward your first cycle. The diagnostic includes a nine-point movement screen:

  • Overhead Squat Assessment — compensations in ankles, knees, hips, and shoulders under a loaded pattern
  • Single-Leg Squat — unilateral stability, hip strength, knee tracking
  • Push & Pull Patterns — shoulder mechanics and scapular stability under load
  • Posture Photos — front, side, and back baseline, re-scored quarterly
  • Joint Range of Motion — shoulders, hips, ankles, thoracic spine
  • Grip Strength — a reliable marker of functional strength, tracked monthly
  • Bodystat Body Composition — medical-grade bioimpedance via the Bodystat QuadScan 4000: lean mass, fat mass, hydration
  • Core Stability — plank and anti-rotation testing
  • Gait Analysis — walking mechanics that reveal asymmetries and compensations

Every finding feeds directly into your program. Nothing is generic, and nothing is guessed.

What does a personal trainer in Santaluz cost?

OmniFit Performance publishes its rates in full. New clients start on The Foundation: three consecutive 4-week cycles paid in full at enrolment, $560 per cycle for studio Momentum, $1,100 for studio Performance, $800 for in-home Momentum, $850 for studio couples and $1,250 for in-home couples. After that, or by direct entry, The Program bills every 4 weeks: $640 to $1,800 at the studio, $900 to $2,600 in-home, $1,000 to $2,900 for couples. Without a program, single sessions are $205 to $275 and 5-packs run $180 to $250 per session depending on setting and membership. The fully virtual Executive Reset runs $200 to $1,100 per 4 weeks. Every client starts with a $150 Performance Assessment, credited toward the first cycle. Note: studio programs require a Teqneeq membership ($119 per month, billed by Teqneeq), with facility access on non-training days included. Full tables on the training rates page.

Do I need a fully equipped home gym?

No. We bring professional-grade portable equipment when needed, so all you need is about 8×8 feet of clear space. That said, most Santaluz clients have substantial setups: power racks, cable machines, cardio equipment, and we design your program around whatever you have.

What is the Performance Assessment?

The OmniFit Performance Assessment ($150) uses the Bodystat QuadScan 4000, a medical-grade bioimpedance device, to establish your body composition baseline: fat mass, lean mass, and hydration markers. Combined with a 9-point movement screen, it gives us objective data to build your program from and to measure real progress against at rescans. The $150 Performance Assessment is credited toward your first program, Reset cycle or 5-pack.

What certifications does your trainer hold?

Nemezio LopezPerez holds NASM-CPT, NASM-CES (Corrective Exercise Specialist), NASM-PES (Performance Enhancement Specialist), NASM-BCS (Behavior Change Specialist), NASM-CNC (Certified Nutrition Coach), and the ACE Corrective Exercise Specialist credential, plus Precision Nutrition certifications. All programming is evidence-based and assessment-driven.

Will this help my golf game?

Directly. Golf demands thoracic rotation, hip mobility, and core stability, all of which deteriorate with desk work and age. Our corrective-first approach restores the mobility your swing needs while building the rotational power and anti-extension strength that protects your lower back. Clients track their progress against their own baseline rather than a fixed timeline.

How is OmniFit different from the trainers at the club?

Club trainers typically serve many members with general programming in a shared space. OmniFit provides fully individualized, assessment-driven training in the privacy of your own home. Your program is built from a $150 Performance Assessment and a 9-point movement screen specific to your body, tracked with monthly data reports, and adjusted based on measurable progress, not general fitness trends.
