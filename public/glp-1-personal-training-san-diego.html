Training on a GLP-1: Keep the Muscle

Strength training and nutrition coaching for clients on semaglutide or tirzepatide. Built around the one thing the medication cannot do for you.

The Short Version

In Short

  • Who it's for: people on semaglutide or tirzepatide who want to keep their muscle while the weight comes off.
  • What happens: resistance training first, protein prioritized inside a suppressed appetite, and body composition measured rather than assumed.
  • Scope: your physician manages the medication. OmniFit trains and never prescribes.

A GLP-1 makes you lose weight. It does not decide what kind of weight. Across published trials, the proportion of weight lost that came from lean mass has ranged from roughly a quarter to nearly half. Resistance training is the intervention that shifts that ratio. At OmniFit Performance the program is built around it: progressively challenging resistance training, adjusting load, repetition ranges, volume and recovery to your current strength and tolerance, protein prioritized inside a suppressed appetite, and body composition measured rather than assumed. Your physician manages the medication. This is the training and eating that runs alongside it.

The Problem

What the Scale Does Not Tell You

The weight comes off. That part is not in question, and for many people it is life-changing. The question nobody asks at the pharmacy counter is what the weight is made of.

Put those two facts together and the risk becomes obvious. If a meaningful share of what you lose is muscle, and most of the weight returns after you stop, you can finish the cycle lighter than you started and worse off in composition than when you began. Same scale number, less muscle underneath it.

That is not an argument against the medication. It is an argument for what you do alongside it, and it is entirely within your control.

The Approach

How the Training Changes

Training someone in a large calorie deficit with a suppressed appetite is not the same job as training someone eating at maintenance. Three things shift.

01

Resistance work is the priority, not the accessory

Conditioning is not the point here. The medication is already producing the deficit. What the training has to do is give the body a reason to keep the muscle it has, and that signal comes from loading it. Cardio gets a supporting role rather than a leading one.

02

Progressive load, matched to you

Progressively challenging resistance training, adjusting load, repetition ranges, volume and recovery to your current strength and tolerance. Light weights feel appropriate when you are eating less and have less energy, but the load has to be meaningful, which means the program has to be built around what you can actually recover from.

03

Sessions built around a smaller energy budget

You have less fuel available and recovery takes longer. Volume gets managed accordingly, sessions get spaced to what you can absorb, and the plan adjusts to how the week actually went rather than what the template says.

Nutrition

Eating When You Are Not Hungry

This is the part most people are unprepared for. The appetite suppression that makes the medication work is the same thing that makes hitting a protein target difficult. You are not going to eat more. So the question becomes what goes into the smaller amount you will eat.

Eat what you can, and make it count. In the early weeks, an open-minded approach matters more than a rigid one. Training tends to raise appetite, and that window is worth using. When you do feel like eating, the choice in that moment is what protects lean mass, and it is a coachable decision rather than a willpower problem.

Practically that means protein-first structures, food choices selected for density rather than volume, and photo logging in the OmniFit app so we can see what actually went in rather than what you remember. Full detail on the coaching approach is on the nutrition coaching page.

Measurement

You Cannot Manage What You Do Not Measure

Body composition, not bodyweight

Bioelectrical impedance analysis estimates fat mass and lean mass; it does not measure muscle directly. On a GLP-1 that distinction is the entire point, and a bathroom scale cannot make it. The Bodystat QuadScan 4000 reads at four frequencies — 5, 50, 100 and 200 kHz — so each rescan reports estimated lean mass and body cell mass beside fat mass, and separates intracellular from extracellular water, which is what keeps a shift in fluid from reading as a change in tissue. Rescans on the same device track the trend over time and show which compartment a change most likely came from. What to do about it is a programming conversation, and a clinical one belongs with your prescriber.

Strength benchmarks every session

Loads logged in the app. Strength that holds or climbs while weight falls is an encouraging sign. We read it alongside repeated body-composition estimates, recovery and training performance, not as proof of muscle retention. If the numbers slide, something in the plan changes before the loss compounds.

A progress review every 4 weeks

A 12-metric framework combining measured performance and self-reported well-being, across seven categories, reviewed formally every 4 weeks (at least every 12 weeks on Train Only) rather than eyeballed. How progress is measured.

Common Questions

Questions About Training on a GLP-1

Do you lose muscle on semaglutide or tirzepatide?

Published trials show a meaningful share of the weight lost comes from lean mass. In the STEP-1 semaglutide DXA analysis it was 45.2% of total weight lost; in SURMOUNT-1 with tirzepatide it was 25.7%, and reported figures across trials range from roughly 15% to 60%. The proportion is not fixed. Resistance training and adequate protein are the two levers that move it, and both are within your control while the medication does its own job.

What happens to my body composition if I stop?

In the STEP-1 trial extension, participants regained 11.6 of the 17.3 percentage points of body weight they had lost within 52 weeks of stopping, and most cardiometabolic improvements moved back toward baseline. Regained weight is not obliged to return as muscle. That is why what you build during the cycle matters more than what the scale says at the end of it.

How should I train while on a GLP-1?

Resistance training first, progressively challenging and adjusted (load, repetition ranges, volume and recovery) to your current strength and tolerance, with conditioning in a supporting role rather than a leading one. The medication is already creating the calorie deficit, so the training's job is to give the body a reason to keep the muscle it has. Volume and session spacing are managed around a smaller energy budget and slower recovery.

How do I hit protein when I have no appetite?

You do not solve it by eating more, because you will not. You solve it by changing what goes into the smaller amount you do eat: protein-first meal structures, foods chosen for density rather than volume, and using the window after training when appetite tends to rise. Photo logging in the OmniFit app means the coaching starts from what actually went in rather than what you remember eating.

Can you tell me whether I should go on a GLP-1?

No. That is a medical decision and it belongs to a licensed provider. OmniFit Performance does not prescribe, recommend, or advise on GLP-1 medication, dosing, side effects, or interactions, and holding fitness certifications in the area does not change that. If you are considering one, speak to a physician first. If you are already on one, the training and nutrition are built around what they have put in place.

Do you have experience with clients on these medications?

Yes. This is established rather than new territory here, and it informed the decision to complete three Eden certifications covering GLP-1 Fitness, GLP-1 Nutrition and Peptide Fundamentals through clinical collaboration. The practical knowledge and the formal credential both sit behind the programming.

Can training on a GLP-1 be HSA or FSA eligible?

In some cases. Personal training can qualify as a medical expense when a licensed provider issues a Letter of Medical Necessity linking it to a diagnosed condition. Eligibility is determined by that provider, not by OmniFit Performance, and reimbursement rests with your plan administrator. See how HSA and FSA eligibility works.

Should I strength train while on a GLP-1 medication?

For most people, yes, once your prescriber agrees. The medication creates the deficit; resistance training gives your body a reason to keep the muscle it has. OmniFit trains and never prescribes: decisions about the medication, its dose and its side effects stay with your prescriber, and the training is coordinated around what they have put in place. If a condition limits exercise, get clearance first.
