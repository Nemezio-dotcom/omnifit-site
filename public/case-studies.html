01 The Challenge

At 6'0" and 145.9 lb, this client started with a BMI of 19.8 and an FFMI of 16.5 — underweight for his frame and a textbook hardgainer. He had a demanding schedule and no appetite for tracking macros. The realistic constraint was one coached session per week, which most programs treat as too little to build on.

02 The OmniFit Plan

  • One coached strength session per week, 12 weeks
  • Progressive overload on compound patterns, no conditioning block
  • No macro tracking — ad libitum intake
  • A single behavioural cue: one extra palm-sized portion of protein daily
  • Bodystat 1500 MDD scans at baseline and week 12

03 Why It Matters

Scale weight cannot tell you what you gained. A 10.6 lb increase means nothing without knowing the split. Bioelectrical impedance analysis recorded 8.2 lb of that as lean mass, with body fat percentage moving 0.4 points and waist circumference unchanged at 33 inches. The waist measurement is the credibility anchor: the gain went where it was programmed to go.

Summary

An anonymized male client, 29, 6'0", gained 10.6 lb of bodyweight over 12 weeks of OmniFit Performance coaching in San Diego on one coached session per week with no macro tracking. Bioelectrical impedance analysis on a Bodystat 1500 MDD recorded 8.2 lb of that gain as lean mass — 77% of the total — with body fat percentage moving from 16.5% to 16.9% and waist circumference unchanged at 33 inches. Fat-free mass index rose from 16.5 to 17.6 and measured basal metabolic rate from 1,720 to 1,823 kcal. Strength progressed across every tracked lift, including bench press from 65 lb for 7 reps to 95 lb for 5. Individual result, measured against this client's own baseline.
