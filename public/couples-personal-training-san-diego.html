Couples Personal Training in San Diego

One session, two people, two separate programs. Train together at Teqneeq Functional Health Center in 4S Ranch, or in your home across North County San Diego.

The Short Version

In Short

  • OmniFit Performance is a diagnostic-led coaching system that takes busy professionals over 30 from pain or plateau to lasting, independent strength.
  • Who it's for: couples who want to train together, each on their own program.
  • What happens: two separate Performance Assessments, two programs, and one split session where you alternate through different work and each log your own numbers.
  • Where: Teqneeq Functional Health Center in 4S Ranch, or your home.
  • Next step: a 45-minute consultation by video or phone. If it's a fit, the Performance Assessment comes next.

Couples training is one coached session with both of you in it. It is not two people doing the same workout. Both partners are screened separately and get their own program. Where goals or physical capacity differ, which is most of the time, the session is split: you alternate through different work rather than sharing a set. What you share is the hour, the coach, and the habit of showing up. Rates are per couple, not per person.

How It Runs

What Actually Happens in the Hour

The most common misunderstanding about couples training is that both people do the same thing at different weights. That works if two people happen to have matching goals, matching injury histories and matching training ages. In practice that is rare.

01

Two separate screens, two separate programs

Each partner completes their own Performance Assessment and gets programming built from their own baseline. Nobody inherits a plan designed for the other person.

02

The session is split

Where goals or capacity differ, you work through different movements on an alternating rhythm. One person works while the other recovers or sets up. Coaching attention moves between you rather than being split down the middle at every moment.

03

You log your own numbers

Both partners track loads and reps in the OmniFit app during the session. In a duo, a coach recording two sets of numbers from across the room produces a worse record than each person entering their own. It also means you each own your progression and can see it rather than being told about it.

04

Progress is measured separately

Two baselines, two sets of benchmarks, two progress reports. Shared sessions do not mean shared metrics.

Who It Works For

Three Situations Where This Fits

One calendar between you

Spouses and partners where two separate appointments were never going to survive the quarter. Booking one slot instead of two is the difference between training and intending to.

Colleagues and business partners

People who already train and want the schedule to hold. The standing appointment does more for adherence than motivation does.

A parent and an adult child

Different decades, different goals, same hour. This is the setup the split-session format handles best, because the programming was never going to be shared anyway.

Honest Limits

When Duo Training Does Not Work

Two people can have different goals and different capacities and still train well together. That is the normal case and the format handles it. What the format does not handle is a gap so wide that one person is effectively training alone in the room.

  • Vastly different physical capacity. If one partner is coming back from surgery and the other is training for a race, the hour cannot serve both. One to one for at least the first block, then reassess.
  • Vastly different goals in opposite directions. Aggressive fat loss alongside a serious strength build pulls programming, nutrition and session structure in different directions.
  • One person is not there by choice. Duo training is not a way to get someone else started. If one of you is being brought along, it usually ends with both of you stopping.
  • You want individual attention for the full hour. That is one to one, and it is the honest recommendation when it is what you actually need.

If any of that describes your situation, I will say so on the consultation rather than after you have paid.

Common Questions

Questions About Couples Training

Do both of us do the same workout?

Usually not. Both partners are screened separately and get their own program, and where goals or physical capacity differ the session is split so you work through different movements on an alternating rhythm. Sharing an hour does not mean sharing a plan. If your goals and capacities happen to line up closely, more of the session overlaps, but that is an outcome of the screens rather than the starting assumption.

What if we are at completely different fitness levels?

A moderate gap is normal and the split-session format handles it well. A very wide gap does not work, and that is the main reason a couple gets turned down. If one partner is returning from surgery while the other is training for performance, the hour cannot serve both properly. The honest recommendation there is one to one for at least the first block, then revisit duo once you are closer.

How much does couples personal training cost in San Diego?

OmniFit Performance couples rates are per couple, not per person. At Teqneeq Functional Health Center in 4S Ranch, The Program runs $1,000 per 4 weeks for Momentum and $2,000 for Performance. In-home runs $1,450 and $2,900. New couples start on The Foundation: three consecutive 4-week cycles at $850 per cycle studio, $1,250 in-home. Teqneeq membership at $119 per month is required for both partners at Momentum and above.

Do we each get our own assessment?

Yes. Each partner completes their own Performance Assessment: a movement screen, resting heart rate and blood pressure, girth measurements and body composition. Two baselines, two sets of benchmarks, two progress reports. The sessions are shared, the measurement is not.

Why do we log our own weights and reps?

Both partners track loads and reps in the OmniFit app during the session. In a duo, one coach recording two people's numbers from across the room produces a worse record than each person entering their own as they go. It also means each of you owns your progression and can see it directly rather than being told about it afterward.

Can we bring a third person?

An extra guest can join any session for $90. That works for an occasional friend or family member. If three people want to train together regularly, that is a different conversation and worth raising on the consultation, since the split-session format has a practical limit.

What if one of us has to miss a session?

The session runs. Whoever is there trains, and it counts as that session for the program. Sessions canceled with less than 24 hours notice are forfeited, which applies to duos the same way it applies to individual clients.

Where does couples training take place?

At Teqneeq Functional Health Center in 4S Ranch, a members-only functional health facility rather than a commercial gym floor, or in your home across North County San Diego with all equipment provided. Studio and in-home rates differ; both are listed above.

Can we train together if we have different goals?

Yes; that is the normal case. Each of you gets your own Performance Assessment and a program built from your own baseline, so one partner can train for fat loss while the other trains for strength. Where goals differ, the session is split: you alternate through different work rather than sharing a set. The limit is a very wide gap in capacity, not different goals.
