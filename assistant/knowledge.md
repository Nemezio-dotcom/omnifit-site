# OmniFit Performance: public fact sheet

- Private 1:1 coaching in three formats: in-studio at Teqneeq (a members-only functional health facility; OmniFit is a trainer operating at Teqneeq, not a studio of its own), in-home across North County San Diego, and live virtual coaching anywhere in the United States.

## Who it is for

- Busy professionals 30+ managing demanding schedules: executives and founders, desk-bound and remote professionals, active adults 50+, people returning after a long break or after physical therapy, people burned by bootcamps or big gyms, and couples.
- People who want joint-friendly strength, better posture, sustainable fat loss and structured accountability.
- People with joint issues or an injury history who need corrective-first training.
- Travelers who need a program that works anywhere.
- Someone who just wants to train is welcome: "Even if you just want to train, we can start there." Train Only is a setting, not a lesser program.

## Who it is not for

- People looking for drop-in classes or group workouts. OmniFit is private 1:1 coaching only.
- People who want the cheapest option available.
- People looking for a 6-week quick fix or crash program.
- People who prefer to train without coaching feedback or accountability, or who are not willing to follow a structured plan between sessions.
- In-home clients outside the in-home service area. For them, the virtual Executive Reset is the option.

## How it works

1. Consultation. A 45-minute consultation by video or phone, booked with a $30 deposit, refunded the same day you attend your consultation. It is a conversation, not an assessment: no movement testing and no measurements.
2. Performance Assessment, if OmniFit is a fit. In person at Teqneeq or in your home: $150. It covers the 9-point movement screen, Bodystat QuadScan 4000 body composition, blood pressure, SpO2, grip strength, posture photos and circumference measurements, and sets the baselines for the 12-metric progress framework. The Virtual Performance Assessment for remote and Executive Reset clients is $75: self-measured with live video guidance, posture photos in the app, no Bodystat scan. Either assessment is credited toward your first purchase: a Foundation or Program cycle, an Executive Reset cycle, or a 5-session pack. Programs and session packs start with a Performance Assessment; for a single session it is recommended, not required. A client returning after a lapse of 60 days or more books a new assessment.
3. Programming. New clients usually start on The Foundation. Experienced clients may start directly on The Program at any tier. Remote clients use the Executive Reset. Single sessions and 5-packs are available without a program. Program graduates can move to the Tune-Up.

Start dates depend on current availability and are confirmed on the consultation. Appointments are confirmed only when payment is received; no slot is held without payment.

## The Accountability Dial

Included at every tier of The Foundation and The Program, and every Executive Reset tier with live sessions, at no price difference. Chosen at the Performance Assessment and changeable at any 4-week cycle boundary, including during The Foundation.

- Full Accountability (the default for the first cycles): daily logging of nutrition, steps and sleep; a coach reply within 1 business day; behavior-change coaching.
- Coached: a weekly written check-in with a coach reply; 1 to 3 habit targets; a measurement review every 4 weeks.
- Train Only: sessions plus the app program; a one-tap weekly check-in; nutrition on request.
- Executive Reset Bronze offers Coached or Train Only.
- The usual path: Full Accountability, then Coached, then Train Only, then the Tune-Up (graduation, not a fourth setting).
- "Tracking is a diagnostic tool, not a life sentence. We measure closely at the start so we can coach less over time. The goal is a client who no longer needs us every week."
- At every setting: payment confirms booking, 24-hour reschedule notice, corrective work in every warm-up, and reassessment at least every 12 weeks.

## The OmniFit Method

The OmniFit Method rests on three pillars, carried through four phases: Assess, Correct, Build, Sustain.

- Measured Progress: every program begins with a Performance Assessment, and progress is judged against your own baseline on a fixed cadence, not against an average.
- Joint-First Strength: corrective work in every warm-up, joint-friendly loading under a 3/10 pain ceiling, and desk-posture work programmed directly.
- Accountability That Travels: the Accountability Dial, studio, in-home and virtual on one app, pause weeks, and the Executive Reset.
- Sessions are for form, load and corrective work. The app carries your individual program between sessions, your logs and your check-ins, written for you rather than a template.

## How OmniFit differs from a gym or a class

- A gym gives access and you write your own program; a group class runs one pace for the room. OmniFit writes your program from your Performance Assessment and rebuilds it every 4-week cycle.
- Measurement: Bodystat QuadScan 4000, nine movement benchmarks, posture photos and strength, against your own baseline.
- Joints and old injuries are programmed for: corrective work in every warm-up and loading chosen for what your joints tolerate.
- Between sessions, your program, logs and check-ins live in one app at the accountability setting you chose.
- When life changes: pause weeks, a Dial you can turn down, tiers you can change between cycles, and the 30-Day Fit Guarantee.

## Injuries, pain and health

- OmniFit trains; it does not diagnose, treat or assess any medical condition, and it is not a medical provider. "Physical therapy treats; OmniFit trains. We pick up where care ends, coordinate with your clinician when you allow it, and never diagnose or treat."
- OmniFit works alongside external physical therapists and chiropractors, such as Stoke Physical Therapy, and may ask for clearance from your clinician before training.
- Movement is screened at the Performance Assessment, never on the consultation.
- Information on the site and in coaching is educational, not medical advice. Consult your physician before beginning any exercise or nutrition program.
